Master Chronological Timeline (1960–1994)

Era 1: 1960–1978 • Early Life & First Homicide
May 21, 1960 Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer is born in Milwaukee, Wisconsin to Lionel and Joyce Dahmer. Vital Statistics Establishes early developmental background. View Early Life Analysis →
June 18, 1978 Disappearance of Steven Mark Hicks (18). Picked up hitchhiking in Bath Township, Ohio; killed at the Dahmer family home. Summit County Sheriff First offense; remained an unsolved missing-persons case until 1991. View Hicks Biography →
Era 2: 1979–1986 • Military Service & Early Legal Contacts
1979 – Mar 1981 U.S. Army Service as medical specialist in Baumholder, West Germany. Incurred repeated disciplinary notices for severe alcohol abuse. United States Army Discharged honorably under administrative provisions for alcohol abuse. Examine Military Records →
1982 – 1986 Relocates to West Allis, Wisconsin to live with grandmother Catherine Dahmer. Arrested for disorderly conduct and indecent exposure. West Allis Police Early municipal police encounters; received fines and suspended sentences.
Era 3: 1987–1989 • West Allis Killings & Court-Ordered Probation
Sep 15, 1987 Disappearance of Steven Tuomi (28) following encounter at the Ambassador Hotel. Milwaukee Police Resumption of homicides after nine-year gap. View Tuomi Biography →
Sep 26, 1988 Arrest for sexual assault of 13-year-old Sombat Sinthasomphone at West Allis residence. West Allis Police Direct legal intervention; formally charged with second-degree sexual assault.
May 23, 1989 Sentencing by Judge William Gardner: 1 year in Milwaukee County House of Correction (work release) + 5 years probation. Milwaukee County Court Critical institutional turning point; allowed unsupervised daily work release. Read Probation Analysis →
Era 4: 1990 • Relocation to Oxford Apartments (Apt 213)
May 14, 1990 Dahmer moves into Apartment 213, Oxford Apartments (924 N. 25th St., Milwaukee). Building Management Oxford Apartments became the site of 12 subsequent killings. View Oxford Context →
May 20, 1990 Raymond Lamont Smith (33) killed in Apartment 213. None First homicide committed inside Apartment 213.
Era 5: 1991 • Escalation, Failed Police Intervention & Discovery
May 27, 1991 Konerak Sinthasomphone Incident: 14-year-old escapes into street; MPD officers Balcerzak, Gabrish, and Rao return him to Dahmer despite bystander objections. MPD Officers / 911 Dispatch Pivotal institutional failure resulting in nationwide investigation and civic outrage. View Complete Incident Analysis →
July 22, 1991 Tracy Edwards escapes Apt 213 with a dangling handcuff and flags down MPD patrolmen Robert Mueller and Rolf Mueller. MPD Patrol Unit Arrest of Dahmer; subsequent discovery of photographic and physical evidence in Apt 213. Read Arrest Narrative →
Era 6: 1992–1994 • Trial, Incarceration & Death
Jan 30 – Feb 15, 1992 Trial on Sanity Phase before Judge Laurence Gram Jr. Jury rejects the NGI (insanity) defense 10-2. Sentenced to 15 consecutive life terms. Milwaukee Circuit Court Landmark legal test of forensic psychiatry under Wisconsin statute. View Trial Proceedings →
Nov 28, 1994 Jeffrey Dahmer is assaulted and killed by inmate Christopher Scarver at Columbia Correctional Institution in Portage, Wisconsin. Wisconsin Dept of Corrections Conclusion of custody; post-mortem legal disputes over brain preservation. Read Final Conclusion →
