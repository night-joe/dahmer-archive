  * **FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 62** (FD-705 Airtel form, dated August 7, 1991):

### 3\. Steven Tuomi: Detective-Attributed DOB vs. Reported Age \& Alleged Offense Date

* **Direct Inspection Findings:**

  * **FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 43** (DE FBIMW 0001, Page Two, teletype dated August 5, 1991):  
`"MILWAUKEE DETECTIVES BELIEVE THAT (X)STEVEN W. TUOMI, WHITE MALE, DATE OF BIRTH OF 12/19/62, LAST KNOWN ADDRESS OF 1315 NORTH CASS, APARTMENT 107, MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN(X), MAY BE ONE OF DAHMER'S VICTIMS. DETECTIVES ADVISED THAT TUOMI WAS LAST SEEN ON 9/15/87 IN MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN, AND HAS NOT BEEN SEEN NOR HAS HE CONTACTED HIS FAMILY SINCE THAT DATE."` (Corroborated on Part 01, PDF p. 53).
  * **FBI Vault Part 03, PDF Page 10** (FD-302, Page 8, interview of Dahmer on August 3, 1992):  
`"DAHMER stated that his second murder was STEVEN TUOMI, and this murder took place at the AMBASSADOR HOTEL somewhere around 1986 or 1987. DAHMER stated that he did not intend to kill TUOMI, but he had passed out and when he came to the next day he was laying on top of TUOMI, and TUOMI was dead. DAHMER realized that he had killed TUOMI. DAHMER then needed to dispose of the body, so he went out and purchased a large suitcase and came back to the AMBASSADOR HOTEL and then placed the body in the suitcase and called a cab... drove DAHMER to his grandmother's house in West Allis, where DAHMER subsequently dissected and disposed of the body."`
  * **FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Pages 3 and 15** (MPD evidence transmittal letters):  
Documents offense series at 924 N. 25th St., Apt. 213 as occurring `"Between November, 1987 and July 19, 1991."`
* **Resolution \& Decision:**

  * Age calculation: Born December 19, 1962. On September 15, 1987 (last-seen date), Tuomi was **24 years, 8 months, 27 days old** (Age 24).
  * Contemporary wire reports (UPI) and retrospective reporting (MJS) give his age as 28. That age is consistent with his being 28 in 1991, roughly four years after his reported last sighting. This is one example of press reporting reflecting age during the later investigation period, not proof that all reported-age discrepancies share the same cause.
  * Legal and offense chronology: Dahmer's confession admits a homicide at the Ambassador Hotel "somewhere around 1986 or 1987." Because no physical remains were ever located, District Attorney E. Michael McCann determined there was insufficient evidence to establish corpus delicti / probable cause in court; hence, no criminal charge was filed in Milwaukee County.
  * **Specification Decision:** Retain displayed age as **`24\\\*`** (calculated from detective-attributed DOB on last-known date). Preserve September 15, 1987 as the reported last-seen date. Document the reported age of 28, the confession's date uncertainty ("around 1986 or 1987"), and the prosecutorial charging rationale citing WaPo \[16] and UPI \[15].

### 4\. Curtis Straughter: Feb 18 vs. March 7 Sighting; Age 17 vs. 18

* **Direct Inspection Findings:**

  * **FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 25** (Teletype DE FBIMW 0008, p. 3):  
`"10) (X)CURTIS STRAUGHTER - BLACK MALE, DOB 4/16/73(X). POSITIVELY IDENTIFIED THROUGH DENTAL RECORDS."`
  * **FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Page 11** (MPD summary, Item #8):  
`"#8., Curtis STRAUGHTER, B/M, dob: 4-16-73, of 3628 N. 19th St., Milwaukee, WI. He was last seen at home by his grandmother on February 18, 1991. He had not been reported missing until \\\[officers] interviewed her on July 24, 1991... We have recovered the skull of Curtis STRAUGHTER."`
  * **Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint (p. 1, 5)**:

    * Count 05 (p. 1): Charges homicide occurring *"on or about February 18, 1991."*
    * Witness statement (p. 5, Entry 1): Grandmother Katherine Straughter swore under oath that she *"last saw her grandson on February 18th, 1991."*
  * **Contemporary Reporting:** UPI (July 26, 1991 \[10]) reports Straughter as age 18 at dental identification.
  * **Retrospective Reporting:** MJS \[1] reports age 18 and last seen March 7, 1991.
* **Resolution \& Decision:**

  * Age calculation: Born April 16, 1973.

    * On February 18, 1991: **17 years, 10 months, 2 days** (Age 17).
    * On March 7, 1991: **17 years, 10 months, 19 days** (Age 17).
    * On July 26, 1991 (identification date): He had passed his 18th birthday on April 16, 1991, making him **18 years old**.
  * Sighting discrepancy: The grandmother's sworn statement in the complaint and the MPD record establish a last physical contact date of February 18, 1991. The March 7 date originates in family/retrospective accounts.
  * **Specification Decision:** Keep displayed age as **`17\\\*`** (DOB-derived age on last-known date). Keep the date cell as `February 18 / March 7, 1991`. In the audit note, explain that age 18 in UPI and MJS reflects his age at identification/death notification after his April 1991 birthday, and clearly contrast the grandmother's sworn Feb 18 sighting against the retrospective March 7 date.

### 5\. Anthony Sears: March 25 Last-Seen vs. March 26 Encounter \& Easter Chronology

* **Direct Inspection Findings:**

  * **FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 24** (Teletype DE FBIMW 0008, p. 2):  
`"5) (X)ANTHONY L. SEARS - BLACK MALE, DOB 1/28/65(X). POSITIVELY IDENTIFIED THROUGH DENTAL RECORDS AND DEFENDANT."`
  * **FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Page 12** (MPD summary, Item #10):  
`"#10. Anthony SEARS, B/M, dob: 1-28-65, of 1557 N. Astor, #7. He was last seen at home on March 25, 1989, at 9:30 A.M., and reported missing to the Milwaukee Police Department by his friend on March 29, 1989... believed to have been murdered at West Allis, WI... We have recovered and identified the skull of Anthony SEARS."`
  * **Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint (p. 1, 3)**:

    * Count 01 (p. 1): Charges homicide occurring *"on or about March 26, 1989."*
    * Witness narrative (p. 3): Friend Jeffrey Connor stated that he and Sears were at a tavern on the evening of March 25, 1989; after closing (early hours of March 26), Sears met an individual named Jeff and they departed.
  * **UPI Contemporary Report (August 16, 1991 \[14])**:  
Reports that police placed Sears's meeting with Dahmer on the night of March 26, 1989, and notes that Sears had told his mother he would be home for Easter on March 27.
  * **Calendar Reconciliation:**  
In 1989, Western Easter Sunday fell on **March 26, 1989** (not March 27).
* **Resolution \& Decision:**

  * Age calculation: Born January 28, 1965 -> On March 25–26, 1989 he was **24 years, 1 month, 25 days** (Age 24).
  * Chronology sequence: Sears was last seen at home on the morning of March 25 (9:30 AM); went to a bar that evening with Connor; met Dahmer around closing time in the early morning hours of March 26; the complaint formally alleges homicide "on or about March 26, 1989." The UPI statement that Sears planned to visit for "Easter on March 27" represents a journalistic date error or conflation with Easter Monday.
  * **Specification Decision:** Keep displayed age as **`24`** (no marker needed; DOB fully supports age 24). Keep last-known date as `March 25, 1989 \\\[1]`. In the audit notes, explicitly record the timeline layers: March 25 home sighting \& evening bar outing; March 26 early morning encounter \& alleged offense date; March 29 missing report; and the calendar discrepancy regarding Easter.
