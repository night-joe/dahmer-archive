## Verified Findings

### 1. Scope, Methodology & Contemporary Press Corpus (July–August 1991 Onwards)

To establish an evidence chain free from secondary distortion, this audit conducted a systematic cross-examination of contemporary press reports published immediately following Jeffrey Dahmer's arrest on July 22, 1991. The contemporary press corpus includes major wire services and regional/national print journalism:
1. **Wire Services:** United Press International (UPI Archives) and Associated Press (AP News / Marquette University wire archives).
2. **Local Milwaukee Daily Press:** *The Milwaukee Journal* (afternoon daily) and *The Milwaukee Sentinel* (morning daily), which provided day-to-day coverage of police press conferences, Medical Examiner briefings, and community reaction.
3. **Regional & National Print Coverage:** *Chicago Tribune*, *Chicago Sun-Times*, *The New York Times*, *The Washington Post*, and the *Los Angeles Times*.

Each report was examined for:
- Exact verbatim article headlines and publication dates (with URL paths and datelines where preserved).
- Victim ages as reported at the time of publication.
- Reported missing-person report dates and last-sighting claims.
- Official forensic identification dates and methods announced by Milwaukee County Medical Examiner Dr. Jeffrey Jentzen and the Milwaukee Police Department (MPD).
- Direct comparison against later retrospective accounts (including the 2022 *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* investigative retrospective `[1]`, the 1992 Court TV sanity trial records `[8]`, and foundational historical accounts by Masters 1993, Norris 1992, and Dvorchak & Holewa 1991).

### 2. Comprehensive Victim-by-Victim Audit (All 17 Victims + Surviving Witness)

#### Entry 0: Tracy Edwards (Surviving Victim & Key Witness)
* **Contemporary 1991 Press Record:**
  * *Associated Press / UPI (July 23, 1991):* "Police Find 11 Bodies in Milwaukee Apartment" / "Suspect Arrested in Multiple Slayings." Reports that late on Monday night, July 22, 1991 (approximately 11:30 P.M.), an unnamed 32-year-old Black man with a pair of handcuffs dangling from his left wrist flagged down MPD Officers Robert Rauth and Rolf Mueller near 25th and State Streets.
  * *The Milwaukee Sentinel / The Milwaukee Journal / UPI (July 24, 1991):* "Suspect in Multiple Slayings Says He 'Responsible'" (UPI). The surviving victim is formally identified as Tracy Edwards, age 32. Contemporary articles report Edwards's statement that Dahmer had lured him and two acquaintances from the Grand Avenue Mall to Apartment 213 at 924 N. 25th Street under the pretense of paying $100 for nude photographs and beer. Edwards stated Dahmer placed a handcuff on one wrist, brandished a large butcher knife, forced him to watch *The Exorcist III*, and stated he intended to eat his heart. Edwards punched Dahmer, bolted through the front door, and brought police back to the apartment.
  * *Milwaukee County Circuit Court Filing (July 25, 1991):* Criminal complaint names Tracy Edwards as the victim of attempted first-degree intentional homicide.
* **Retrospective Accounts:**
  * *Court TV / Trial Record (Jan. 30, 1992):* Edwards testified under oath as the prosecution's first witness at Dahmer's sanity trial, detailing the five hours of terror inside Apartment 213.
  * *Brian Masters (1993), "The Shrine of Jeffrey Dahmer", pp. 5–9:* Documents Edwards's escape as the catalyst that unraveled the 13-year homicide series.
  * *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel (2022) [1]:* Documents Edwards's crucial role in ending the series and notes his subsequent struggles with trauma.
* **Verification Assessment:** **Verified.** Edwards's age was accurately reported as 32 in July 1991; contemporary news reports accurately convey his escape through the apartment door, which was subsequently affirmed in sworn trial testimony.

#### Entry 1: Steven Mark Hicks
* **Contemporary 1991 Press Record:**
  * *The Washington Post (July 29, 1991):* "Dahmer Says First Slaying Was in 1978" by Edward Walsh. Reports that Dahmer confessed to Milwaukee detectives that his first homicide occurred in June 1978 at his parents' home in Bath Township, Ohio, involving an 18-year-old male hitchhiker named Steven Hicks.
  * *The New York Times (July 31, 1991) [4]:* "Bone Fragments, Many Human, Are Found at Suspect's Ohio Home." Reports Bath Township and Summit County authorities executing a search warrant on the Dahmer property; hundreds of bone fragments were excavated from the surrounding woods and yard.
  * *The Washington Post (Sept. 13, 1991; URL path Sept. 14, 1991) [17]:* "Bones Found at Dahmer Site Identified." Reports Summit County Coroner Dr. William A. Cox formally confirmed that excavated bone fragments—specifically a molar tooth, a tooth fragment, and a cervical vertebra—belonged to Steven Mark Hicks, through direct comparison with his family's dental records and childhood X-rays.
  * *The Washington Post (May 2, 1992) [5]:* "Dahmer Gets Life Term in 1978 Murder in Ohio." Coverage of Dahmer's guilty plea and sentencing in Summit County Common Pleas Court, reporting statements by Hicks's father Richard Hicks.
* **Retrospective Accounts:**
  * *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel Retrospective (2022) [1]:* Steven Mark Hicks, age 18. Recent graduate of Coventry High School, Coventry Township, Ohio. Recalls his father Richard Hicks describing him as deeply caring, becoming distraught after shooting a rabbit on a hunting trip. Last seen hitchhiking to Chippewa Lake Park on June 18, 1978.
  * *Brian Masters (1993), pp. 41–48; Joel Norris (1992), pp. 43–47:* Retrospective accounts corroborate the June 18, 1978 date and recount the murder with a barbell after Hicks attempted to leave.
* **Discrepancy Analysis:**
  * *Age at Disappearance:* Age 18 is unanimously verified. FBI records contain two minor DOB variants (June 22, 1959 in Part 03 p. 51 and Part 01 p. 39 vs. June 27, 1959 in Part 01 p. 25). Both dates establish that on June 18, 1978, he was 18 years old (four or nine days before his 19th birthday).
  * *Date Separation:* Last-known contact was June 18, 1978 (FBI Part 03 p. 52). His father reported him missing to the Summit County Sheriff's Department on June 24, 1978 (FBI Part 01 p. 60). Contemporary reporting and retrospective accounts occasionally conflated these dates.
  * *Publication Date Metadata:* WaPo `[17]` displays September 13, 1991 on the article page, but its digital archive URL path displays `1991/09/14`. Both dates are recorded in project metadata.

#### Entry 2: Steven Walter Tuomi
* **Contemporary 1991 Press Record:**
  * *UPI / Associated Press (July 24–26, 1991):* Early wire stories report Dahmer admitted to killing a man at the Ambassador Hotel in downtown Milwaukee in 1987, dismembering the body, and transporting it in a suitcase.
  * *FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 43 (Aug. 5, 1991) & p. 53 (Aug. 8, 1991):* MPD teletypes record that detectives identified Steven W. Tuomi (DOB 12/19/62, last known address 1315 N. Cass St., Apt. 107, Milwaukee) as the suspected 16th victim, noting he was last seen on September 15, 1987 and had not contacted his family since.
  * *UPI (Aug. 6, 1991) [13]:* "Dahmer Charged with Dozen Killings." Identifies Tuomi as 28 years old, last seen in September 1987.
  * *The Washington Post (Aug. 22/23, 1991) [16]:* "Dahmer's Attorney Hints at Insanity Defense." Reports District Attorney E. Michael McCann's formal announcement that Wisconsin murder charges were restricted strictly to victims where physical remains established corpus delicti; because Tuomi's body was never located, no charge could be brought.
  * *UPI (Sept. 11, 1991) [15]:* "Dahmer Opts for Innocent and Insanity Pleas." Confirms Tuomi was omitted from the Milwaukee County indictment due to lack of independent corroborating physical evidence.
* **Retrospective Accounts:**
  * *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel (2022) [1]:* Lists Tuomi as age 28. Grew up in Ontonagon, Michigan; worked as a short-order cook at Marc's Big Boy in Milwaukee. Classmates recalled him as quiet and artistic (noting a stained-glass lamp). Last seen/heard from September 15, 1987.
  * *Brian Masters (1993), pp. 78–83:* Gives the date of the Ambassador Hotel encounter as November 20, 1987 (based on trial disclosures), differing from MPD missing reports.
  * *Dahmer Interrogation (FBI Part 03, PDF p. 10):* Dahmer told detectives on August 3, 1992 that the homicide occurred "somewhere around 1986 or 1987" and that he had blacked out and awoke to find Tuomi dead beside him.
* **Discrepancy Analysis:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (24 vs. 28):* UPI `[13]` and MJS `[1]` report age 28. However, the detective-attributed DOB of December 19, 1962 (FBI Part 01 p. 43) proves that on his September 15, 1987 last-seen date, Tuomi was **24 years, 8 months, 27 days old** (Age 24). The reported age of 28 reflects his chronological age during the July 1991 investigation. Table 1 v04 correctly displays `24*`.
  * *Chronology Conflict:* MPD files and MJS document last contact on September 15, 1987; Dahmer's confession states "around 1986 or 1987"; Masters's retrospective suggests November 1987. The exact date of death remains legally unproven.

   - *Status:* Unresolved. The two dates differ by 17 days. Neither date should be erased. Table 1 preserves both dates explicitly.
2. **Steven Tuomi: Ambassador Hotel Chronology:**
   - MPD teletypes (FBI Part 01 p. 43) record Tuomi last seen on **September 15, 1987**.
   - Dahmer stated in an official FD-302 interview (FBI Part 03 p. 10) that the murder occurred "somewhere around 1986 or 1987."
   - Brian Masters (1993) places the murder on **November 20, 1987**.
   - *Status:* Unresolved. Because no physical remains were recovered and no charges were filed, the precise date of death is legally and historically indeterminable.
3. **Anthony Sears: March 25 vs. March 26 Bar Encounter & UPI Easter Error:**
   - Sears was seen at home on the morning of March 25, 1989 (FBI Part 06 p. 12); went to a bar that evening; met Dahmer after closing in the early morning of March 26 (Connor witness statement, Complaint `[3]`).
   - UPI `[14]` places the meeting on the "night of March 26" and reports Sears planned to visit his mother for "Easter on March 27."
   - *Status:* Unresolved reporting defect in UPI. Easter Sunday was March 26, 1989. The profile maintains March 25 as the last-known date and explains the evening-to-morning sequence.
4. **Ernest Miller: September 2 Disappearance vs. September 3 1:30 A.M. Departure:**
   - MJS `[1]` and UPI `[11]` report Miller disappeared Sunday, September 2, 1990.
   - Aunt Vivian Miller swore in Complaint `[3]` that Ernest left her home at 1:30 A.M. on Monday, September 3, 1990.
   - *Status:* Documented date-boundary discrepancy. Table 1 v04 displays `September 2–3, 1990`.
