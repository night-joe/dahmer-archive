| Dahmer placed the event “around 1986 or 1987” in an interview account | FBI record **[2]** | Preserve this uncertainty and do not assert a precise death date. No remains were recovered. |

### Newly identified age discrepancy

The Victims plan follows the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective and presents Tuomi as **28**. The FBI birth date recorded in the verification report is **December 19, 1962**, which would make him **24 on September 15, 1987**, the listed last-seen date. The sources therefore disagree. The existing verification report did not include Tuomi in its formal age-discrepancy list.

**Handling:** Do not silently replace 28 with 24 or describe either age as definitively settled. Add an explicit source note after the FBI image/page is checked and anchored. This audit establishes that the records conflict; it does not establish which record is correct.

### Citation correction

The Victims source directory’s **[4]** is UPI’s July 24, 1991 report about the initial count of victims. That is not the direct source for the reason Tuomi was not charged. Add a specific UPI report, such as:

- UPI, “Dahmer opts for innocent and insanity pleas” (September 11, 1991): https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991-09-11/Dahmer-opts-for-innocent-and-insanity-pleas/1776684561600/

The report discusses the prosecutor’s decision not to file a homicide charge because of insufficient evidence and notes the absence of remains. Verify exact article wording before quoting it in final copy.

A contemporary UPI report identified Doxtator as the 17th victim claimed by Dahmer and stated that he was last seen in January 1988; it described police belief that he was killed shortly after disappearing. An early FBI record contains an estimated killing date of January 18, 1988. That estimate should not replace the January 16 last-seen date in the Victims table.

The early FBI record also contains inconsistent descriptive classifications. Do not use those classifications to validate the profile’s ancestry statement; use the principal biographical source and cautious attribution.
