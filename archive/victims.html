Biographical Record

The Victims

The victims were seventeen young men and teenagers with families, passions, talents, and futures. This section restores attention to who they were as individuals, reconstructing their lives from verified public records, contemporary family recollections, and legal archives.

Core Editorial Principle: Where historical records are thin or dates conflict among contemporary news accounts, this archive preserves the uncertainty rather than fabricating details. The deaths are noted only with the minimum factual context required for historical understanding.

Detailed Biographical Profiles

Steven Mark Hicks

Age 18 • Coventry Township, Ohio

Steven had recently graduated from high school. His father, Richard Hicks, described him as a sensitive, compassionate young man who loved music. He was hitchhiking to a rock concert at Chippewa Lake Park when he disappeared on June 18, 1978.

Anthony Carter Hughes

Age 31 • Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Anthony lost his hearing and speech during infancy due to medical complications. Known to friends as 'Tony,' he communicated with vibrant expressiveness through American Sign Language, was deeply loved by his mother Shirley, and pursued modeling.

Konerak Sinthasomphone

Age 14 • Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Konerak arrived in the United States from Laos as a child with his parents and siblings. On the night of May 27, 1991, he managed to escape into the street, leading to an infamous encounter with police officers who dismissed bystander pleas and returned him to the apartment.

Oliver Joseph Lacy

Age 23 • Chicago, Illinois

Oliver was a dedicated bodybuilder who worked in hospitality. He was engaged to be married and was the proud father of a two-year-old son. Family members described him as protective, disciplined, and full of plans for his young family's future.
