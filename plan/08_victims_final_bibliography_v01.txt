SOURCE VERIFICATION NOTES — OPEN ITEMS PRESERVED
------------------------------------------------
These notes do not rewrite the frozen profile prose. They identify claims whose exact scope or
locator is still pending, so a numbered citation is not mistaken for full verification.

- **Jamie Doxtator:** UPI [14] confirms January 1988 at month-level, not January 16. The exact day
  in the profile remains unverified and needs a direct, page-level source before final publication.
- **Konerak Sinthasomphone’s family arrival:** UPI [6] supports 1980 but not November. The described
  Milwaukee Sentinel clipping is believed to be preserved in FBI Part 04 under [2], but its
  standalone PDF page/image locator is not yet confirmed. The month remains pending.
- **Anthony Sears:** MJS [1] gives March 25 as last seen; UPI [14] describes an encounter on the
  night of March 26 and an Easter-home plan dated March 27. Easter Sunday in 1989 was March 26, so
  the UPI sequence is internally inconsistent. The profile retains the MJS date without claiming
  the UPI dates reconcile.
- **Steven Tuomi:** MJS [1] reports age 28; UPI [13] also gives a contemporary age label, while FBI Part 01 PDF p. 43 records a DOB detectives “believe”
  was December 19, 1962, implying age 24 on September 15, 1987. [1, 2 (Part 01, PDF p. 43; Part 03, PDF p. 10), 13, 15, 16] FBI Part 03 PDF p. 10 describes the
  alleged Ambassador Hotel event as “somewhere around 1986 or 1987.” Keep these as distinct source
  statements; do not claim a precise death date or silently resolve the discrepancy.
- **Jeremiah Weinberger:** FBI Part 01 PDF p. 25 records September 25, 1967, while user-verified FBI
  Part 06 PDF p. 9 records September 29, 1967. Both locators are user-confirmed. A third September 28
  variant previously transcribed has no sufficiently reliable standalone locator in this audit and
  is not treated here as a third confirmed anchor. All dates still yield age 23 in July 1991.
- **Oliver Lacy:** FBI Part 01 PDF p. 24 records June 23, 1968, while user-verified FBI Part 06 PDF
  p. 9 records June 23, 1967. Both are recorded as sourced conflicts; neither is silently selected.
- **Age reference points:** UPI [10] and [11] often report age at identification/publication in 1991,
  not age on the earlier last-seen date. Keep age-at-report and age-at-last-known-contact distinct.
  Guerrero’s UPI [11] age of 23 when reported missing remains in tension with DOB-derived age 22.
- **Ernest Miller and David Thomas:** UPI [11] gives family-reported September disappearance details but
  does not erase the differences between Miller’s September 2 report, the complaint/FBI early
  September 3 record, and Thomas’s exact September 24 last-seen date reported by MJS [1].
- **Curtis Straughter:** MJS [1] reports last seen March 7 and age 18; FBI/complaint records [2, 3]
  give DOB April 16, 1973 and the grandmother’s February 18 last sighting. Keep both chronology
  accounts explicit; do not force them into a reconciled single date.
- **Complaint language:** Source [3] records allegations and sworn statements. Do not present an
  alleged “on or about” offense date as a judicially established exact date.
