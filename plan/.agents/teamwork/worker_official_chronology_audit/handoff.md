## 2. Logic Chain

1. **From Complaint Text to Procedural Sequence:**
   - Direct inspection of the August 6, 1991 Amended Criminal Complaint confirms it is 9 pages long and charges exactly 12 counts (Anthony Sears through Joseph Bradehoft).
   - Because the 12-count complaint omits James Doxtator, Richard Guerrero, and Edward Smith, and contemporary court records confirm Dahmer's preliminary hearing waiver on August 22, 1991 where three additional counts were filed, the Milwaukee County prosecution definitively progressed in three stages: 4 counts (July 25) $\rightarrow$ 12 counts (August 6) $\rightarrow$ 15 counts (August 22).
2. **From Statutory Dates to Legal Nomenclature:**
   - Wisconsin's revision of Wis. Stat. § 940.01 became effective January 1, 1989.
   - Doxtator (Jan 1988) and Guerrero (Mar 1988) were killed prior to January 1, 1989; therefore, prosecutors could only charge them with First-Degree Murder.
   - Sears (Mar 1989) and all subsequent Milwaukee victims were killed after January 1, 1989; therefore, they were charged with First-Degree Intentional Homicide.
   - Conclusion: Describing the prosecution as "15 counts of first-degree intentional homicide" is legally erroneous and must be corrected to 2 counts of First-Degree Murder and 13 counts of First-Degree Intentional Homicide.
3. **From Four Distinct Milestones to Non-Collapsing Mandate:**
   - For Oliver Lacy: Last seen July 12; reported missing July 15; alleged encounter/homicide on or about July 15; identified July 24. Conflating July 12 and July 15 erases the documented 3-day disappearance gap.
   - For Joseph Bradehoft: Last seen July 16; reported missing to Greenville IL PD; alleged encounter/homicide on or about July 19; identified July 25–26. Conflating July 16 and July 19 erases the 3-day disappearance gap.
   - For Jeremiah Weinberger: Reported missing July 6; alleged homicide July 7; Seventh Circuit shorthand in *Weinberger v. Wisconsin* erroneously used "on or about July 6", demonstrating how legal shorthand can collapse distinct milestones.
   - For Curtis Straughter: Sworn eyewitness grandmother statement in complaint p. 5 anchors last sighting to February 18, 1991; retrospective press cited March 7, 1991. Both dates must remain categorized rather than merged.
   - Conclusion: Strict separation across the four milestones (Last-Known Contact, Missing Report, Alleged Offense Date, Official Identification) is essential for factual integrity.
