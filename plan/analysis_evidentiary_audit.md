Code § DOC 328.04 revealed that Chester maintained ~2 face-to-face meetings per month (31 total meetings), monitored outpatient alcohol treatment at DePaul Rehabilitation Hospital, and that monthly residential inspections were explicitly waived in writing by supervisor James Wake due to Dahmer's apparent compliance, steady full-time employment at Ambrosia Chocolate, and negative urinalysis.
3. **The Clinical vs. Legal Insanity Divide:** Popular discourse routinely conflates clinical mental disorder with legal criminal insanity. All evaluating clinicians agreed that Dahmer suffered from severe paraphilia (necrophilia) and alcohol dependence. The 1992 sanity trial before Judge Laurence C. Gram Jr. turned strictly on Wis. Stat. § 971.15(1): whether a mental disease or defect deprived Dahmer of substantial capacity to appreciate wrongfulness or conform his conduct to the law.
4. **Behavioral Addiction Heuristic vs. Diagnostic Fact:** The landmark 2022 study by Adam Lankford and Jenna K. Hayes in *Sexual Health & Compulsivity* applied DSM-5 Substance Use Disorder criteria *by analytical analogy*. The DSM-5 does not recognize "serial murder addiction" as a validated diagnostic category. This distinction must be explicitly maintained to prevent pseudo-scientific misrepresentation.
5. **Withdrawn Scholarly Literature:** The 1994 paper *Destructive Hostility* (Jentzen, Palermo et al.) is documented in Marquette University institutional archives as **WITHDRAWN**. It must be treated strictly as a historical artifact of contemporary debate rather than peer-reviewed consensus.

## 1. Verified Findings

### Section A: Systemic Failures & Institutional Contacts (`06b`)

#### 1. May 27, 1991 Encounter (Konerak Sinthasomphone)
- **Time and Location:** Approximately 1:56 AM – 2:30 AM at the intersection of North 25th Street and West State Street, and inside Apartment 213 of the Oxford Apartments (924 N. 25th St.) [SRC-01: 838 F. Supp. at 1322].
- **Bystander Assistance:** Nicole Childress and Sandra Smith (African American residents) observed 14-year-old Konerak Sinthasomphone naked, bruised, bleeding from the rectum and buttocks, dazed, and unable to speak coherently following a trepanning burr-hole incision and injection of hydrochloric acid by Dahmer [SRC-01, SRC-10: FBI Part 01 p. 24]. (Tina Spivey, Smith's cousin, is noted in contemporary AP wire reports [SRC-17]).
- **Police & Paramedic Dispatch:** MPD Officers John A. Balcerzak and Joseph T. Gabrish (Squad 25) arrived at ~2:00 AM. Officer Richard Porubcan (Squad 27, rookie backup with 4 months of service) responded separately. Milwaukee Fire Department Ambulance 7 / Engine 3 arrived; paramedics evaluated Sinthasomphone and stated he required hospital transport. Officers Balcerzak and Gabrish dismissed the ambulance crew and canceled medical transport [SRC-01: 838 F. Supp. at 1322–1323; SRC-02: 785 F. Supp. at 1346].
- **Dahmer's Deception & Domestic Squabble Narrative:** Dahmer told officers the boy was his 19-year-old adult Asian boyfriend "John Mung" who had consumed too much alcohol. When bystanders protested that the youth was a child and visibly injured, officers threatened bystanders with arrest if they did not clear the street [SRC-01, SRC-02].
- **Apartment 213 Entry:** Officers entered Apartment 213, observed Polaroid photos of Sinthasomphone, and Officer Gabrish detected a foul odor (Tony Hughes's decomposing remains were in the bedroom). Officers failed to demand identification, verify the boy's age, run an MPD computer warrant query on Dahmer (which would have revealed active felony probation for the 1988 child sexual assault of Konerak's older brother Sombat), or search beyond the front room [SRC-01, SRC-06].
- **Subsequent Homicide:** Officers cleared the scene at ~2:30 AM, leaving Sinthasomphone with Dahmer. The federal court record in *Estate of Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee* explicitly finds that "some 30 minutes later," Dahmer injected hydrochloric acid into Konerak's brain and murdered him [SRC-01: 838 F. Supp. at 1323]. Dahmer subsequently killed four more victims before his arrest on July 22, 1991 [SRC-10].
- **Derogatory Radio Transmissions & Follow-Up Calls:**
  - At 2:33 AM, Officer Balcerzak transmitted a recorded radio message stating the incident was "an intoxicated boyfriend and boyfriend" dispute and stated: "It's all taken care of... Need to wash my hands... I have to delouse myself" [SRC-19: UPI Aug 2, 1991].
  - Glenda Cleveland (aunt of Nicole Childress and Sandra Smith) placed recorded calls to the MPD 5th District station. Balcerzak stated: "It was an adult. He's nineteen years old... It's all taken care of, ma'am" and refused to take witness statements [SRC-11: jsonline 2011].

#### 2. Officer Disciplinary Sequence & Reinstatement
- **July 26, 1991:** MPD Chief Philip Arreola suspended Officers Balcerzak, Gabrish, and Porubcan with pay pending internal affairs inquiry [SRC-04: 163 F.3d at 994].
- **September 6, 1991:** Chief Arreola discharged Balcerzak and Gabrish for gross negligence, failure to verify identity, failure to protect a juvenile, and rule violations. Porubcan's discharge was held in abeyance for one year due to inexperience [SRC-04, SRC-18].
- **October 13, 1992:** Balcerzak and Gabrish pleaded guilty to department rule violations before the Milwaukee Board of Fire and Police Commissioners (FPC); the FPC affirmed their terminations [SRC-04, SRC-20].
- **April 27, 1994:** On appeal to state circuit court under Wis. Stat. § 62.13(5)(i), Reserve Circuit Judge Robert Parins ruled discharge was excessively severe, ordered Balcerzak and Gabrish reinstated with a maximum 60-day suspension, and awarded approximately $55,000 in back pay each [SRC-04, SRC-21].
- **Federal Litigation (*Balcerzak v. City of Milwaukee*):** The officers sued under § 1983; on Dec 18, 1998, the Seventh Circuit (*163 F.3d 993*) affirmed summary judgment dismissing their claims [SRC-04]. Balcerzak was elected President of the Milwaukee Police Association in May 2005; retired 2017. Gabrish served as police captain and interim chief in Grafton, WI.

#### 3. Federal Civil Rights Litigation (*Estate of Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee*)
- **March 5, 1992 (785 F. Supp. 1343):** Judge Terence T. Evans denied defendants' motion to dismiss equal protection claims, ruling plaintiffs adequately alleged that police provided less protection to minority and gay victims than to white heterosexuals; noted 11 of 17 victims were Black [SRC-02].
- **November 23, 1993 (838 F. Supp. 1320):** Judge Evans granted qualified immunity on substantive due process under *DeShaney v. Winnebago County* (1989), holding the Due Process Clause does not guarantee protection against private violence, while leaving equal protection claims for trial [SRC-01].
- **April 25, 1995 Settlement:** The Milwaukee Common Council voted 14-2 to approve an $850,000 settlement to the Sinthasomphone family without admitting liability or discrimination [SRC-14, SRC-15].

#### 4. Probation Supervision & Home Visit Waivers (*Weinberger v. Wisconsin*)
- **1989 Sentence:** On May 24, 1989, Judge William D. Gardner sentenced Dahmer for the sexual assault of Sombat Sinthasomphone: 1 year in Milwaukee House of Correction with Huber work-release privileges (at Ambrosia Chocolate, 1133 N. 5th St), followed by 5 years probation with mandatory alcohol treatment and no contact with minors [SRC-03: 105 F.3d at 1184].
- **Supervision Reality (Agent Donna Chester):** Chester conducted 31 in-person meetings (twice monthly), verified outpatient alcohol compliance at DePaul Rehabilitation Hospital, monitored employment, and referred Dahmer to the Milwaukee County Mental Health Complex [SRC-03].
- **The Home-Visit Waiver System:** Under Wis. Admin. Code § DOC 328.04, maximum supervision mandated monthly home visits UNLESS waived in writing by a supervisor. Chester's supervisor, James Wake, signed written waivers exempting home visits due to Dahmer's steady employment, negative drug/alcohol screens, and perfect office compliance [SRC-03].
- **Seventh Circuit Holding (105 F.3d 1182, Jan 23, 1997):** Affirmed summary judgment in favor of Chester and Wake, holding they did not act with deliberate indifference or recklessness. The court noted no evidence showed that monthly home visits would have prevented the concealed murders [SRC-03].

### Section B: Psychological Profile & Evaluation (`06a`)

#### 1. Clinical vs. Legal Insanity Standard (Wis. Stat. § 971.15)
- Under Wisconsin's modified ALI rule, a defendant is not criminally responsible if at the time of conduct, as a result of mental disease or defect, he lacked substantial capacity either to *appreciate the wrongfulness* of conduct (cognitive prong) or to *conform conduct* to the requirements of law (volitional prong) [SRC-07].
- Exclusions: Wis. Stat. § 971.15(2) expressly excludes abnormalities manifested only by repeated criminal/antisocial conduct (antisocial personality disorder). Voluntary alcohol intoxication does not constitute a legal defense.
- Burden of Proof: Defendant must prove mental disease/defect by greater weight of credible evidence (civil preponderance) [SRC-07]. Under Wis. Stat. § 805.09(2), a 5/6 verdict (10 of 12 jurors) is required [SRC-07].

#### 2. Clinical Evaluation Methodology
- Over 200 hours of clinical interviews across six months [SRC-11, SRC-24].
- Psychometric Testing: WAIS-R Full-Scale IQ in the 115–120 range (superior-to-bright normal intellect, preserved abstract reasoning); MMPI elevated on depression, social introversion, and psychopathic deviance, but ruling out active schizophrenia [SRC-11, SRC-28].
- Neurological Testing: Comprehensive EEG, CT/MRI scans normal; no gross organic brain lesions, epilepsy, or focal cerebral pathology [SRC-11, SRC-34].

#### 3. Testifying Expert Witnesses & Sanity Positions
- **Defense Experts:**
  - *Dr. Frederick S. Berlin* (Johns Hopkins): Diagnosed necrophilia as an irresistible mental disease; testified Dahmer suffered overwhelming volitional impairment; conceded Dahmer never attacked in daylight or before witnesses [SRC-25].
  - *Dr. Judith V. Becker* (Univ. of Arizona): Diagnosed necrophilia and paraphilia; conceded on cross-examination that Dahmer released several prospective victims (Ronald Flowers, Luis Sandoval), proving selective behavioral inhibition [SRC-26].
  - *Dr. Carl Wahlstrom* (Rush-Presbyterian): Diagnosed multiaxial pathology (necrophilia, BPD, schizotypal PD, alcohol dependence, psychotic disorder NOS); interpreted trepanning experiments as psychotic attempts to create living companions [SRC-27].
- **Court-Appointed / Independent Experts:**
  - *Dr. George F. Palermo* (Milwaukee County Court Clinic): Diagnosed mixed personality disorder (antisocial, sadomasochistic) and alcohol dependence; testified Dahmer killed out of hostility, lust, power, and terror of rejection; concluded legally sane [SRC-23].
  - *Dr. Samuel H. Friedman* (Court Clinic Psychologist): Conducted testing; characterized Dahmer as solitary predatory hunter with trophy attachment; concluded legally sane [SRC-28].
  - *Dr. Frederick A. Fosdal* (Madison): Diagnosed necrophilia and alcohol dependence; drew vital distinction that necrophilia was a sexual preference/paraphilia, NOT a statutory "mental disease"; concluded legally sane [SRC-29].
- **Prosecution Expert:**
  - *Dr. Park Elliott Dietz* (UCLA): Diagnosed Paraphilia NOS (necrophilia) and alcohol abuse/dependence; established that paraphilia provides the erotic motive but does not impair choice or self-control; detailed forward-looking planning (chemicals, Halcion), instrumental use of alcohol to suppress squeamishness, and selective restraint; concluded legally sane on all counts [SRC-24].

#### 4. Trepanning / Control Experiments
- Skulls drilled in 4 victims (Errol Lindsey, Anthony Sears, Konerak Sinthasomphone, Jeremiah Weinberger) using hand drills, injecting hydrochloric acid or boiling water [SRC-34, SRC-10].
- Clinical consensus: Attempt to produce a permanently compliant, vegetative partner to avoid abandonment and eliminate the burden of continuous killing/disposal [SRC-24, SRC-34].

#### 5. Sanity Verdict (February 15, 1992)
- Counts 1 & 2 (Sears & Smith): 10–2 finding that Dahmer was legally sane [SRC-30].
- Counts 3–15 (Miller through Bradehoft): 11–1 or 12–0 unanimous finding of legal sanity [SRC-30].
- Formally rejected legal insanity defense across all 15 Wisconsin counts [SRC-11, SRC-30].

### Section C: Theories & Motivations (`06c`)

#### 1. Three Epistemological Tiers
- Tier 1: Documented Facts (confessions, crime scene chemicals, autopsy findings, court dockets).
- Tier 2: Clinical Interpretations (trial testimonies of Berlin, Becker, Wahlstrom, Palermo, Fosdal, Dietz).
- Tier 3: Scholarly Hypotheses (Lankford & Hayes 2022 addiction model, Egger's less-dead framework).

#### 2. Seven Competing Motivational Theories
1. *Possession, Control & Companionship:* Primary courtroom theory; freeze-drying inquiry ($10,000+ cost) [SRC-24]; trepanning experiments; bleached skull retention [SRC-10].
2. *Behavioral Addiction / Compulsion Model:* Lankford & Hayes (2022, *Sexual Health & Compulsivity*); systematic mapping to DSM-5 Substance Use Disorder criteria (tolerance, withdrawal, failed cessation) by analogy; 1993 *Inside Edition* interview with Stone Phillips describing urges as "almost addictive"; FBI BSU Vault Part 03 p. 55 unedited tape acquisition [SRC-32, SRC-12, SRC-10].
3. *Alcohol as Instrumental Tool:* Dr. Dietz testimony; alcohol deliberately used to numb moral squeamishness and facilitate dismemberment [SRC-24].
4. *Sexual Gratification & Necrophilia:* Erotic focus on somnophilia, male anatomy, and corpses; clinical consensus across all 7 trial experts [SRC-11].
5. *Fear of Abandonment & Narcissistic Hostility:* Dr. Palermo formulation; fragile narcissism, terror of rejection, preemptive destruction [SRC-23].
6. *Destructive Hostility & Sadism:* Jentzen, Palermo et al. (1994) paper; officially WITHDRAWN in Marquette University institutional repository; treated purely as historical artifact [SRC-36].
7. *Longitudinal Behavioral Escalation across 3 Phases:* Phase 1 (1978 Ohio, 1 victim, panic); Phase 2 (1987–89 West Allis, 4 victims, sedation); Phase 3 (1990–91 Oxford Apts, 12 victims, rapid escalation and collapse) [SRC-34, SRC-10].

## 3. Unresolved Discrepancies & Historiographical Ambiguities

1. **Tina Spivey's Presence on May 27, 1991:**
   Federal court decisions (*785 F. Supp. at 1346*; *838 F. Supp. at 1322*) name Nicole Childress and Sandra Smith as the bystanders who found Konerak Sinthasomphone. Contemporary wire reporting (Marquette AP wire July 26, 1991) and subsequent civil filings also identify Sandra Smith's cousin, Tina Spivey, as being present. Specification `06b` explicitly documents Smith and Childress from the formal judicial findings, while attributing Spivey's presence to contemporary wire dispatches.
2. **Police Radio Voice Identification:**
   While the recording of the 2:33 AM radio transmission ("delouse myself") is authenticated primary evidence, archival audio does not conclusively isolate whether Balcerzak or Gabrish uttered the specific phrase "delouse myself." The locked specification attributes the transmission to Squad 25 / Officer Balcerzak (who operated the cruiser radio) without asserting speculative personal voice attribution.
3. **Number of Living Victims Released:**
   During cross-examination, Dr. Judith Becker acknowledged that Dahmer released multiple living individuals after drugging them. Confession records specifically corroborate Ronald Flowers (drugged at grandmother's house in 1988, woke up in hospital) and Luis Sandoval (released from Oxford Apartments). Other individuals claimed in secondary literature to have been released cannot be conclusively verified in primary police records; the specification cites only Flowers and Sandoval.
