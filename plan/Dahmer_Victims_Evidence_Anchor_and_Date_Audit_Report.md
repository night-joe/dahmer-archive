## 1. Discrepancy & Category Comparison Table

* **Category A:** Last seen / last known alive (eyewitness, family contact, or phone call).
* **Category B:** Officially reported missing to a law enforcement agency.
* **Category C:** Alleged offense date in charging document or confession.
* **Category D:** Forensic identification or next-of-kin notification date.
* **Category E:** Other dated statement of limited or uncertain scope.

## 2. Person-by-Person Audits for Discrepant Dates

### A. Curtis Straughter
1. **Official Record Wording:**
   * *Complaint p. 5, Entry 1:* Sworn statement of grandmother Katherine Straughter that she *"last saw her grandson on February 18th, 1991."* [Category A]
   * *Complaint p. 1, Count 05:* Formal charge alleging homicide occurred *"on or about February 18, 1991."* [Category C]
   * *FBI Part 06, p. 11:* Confirms grandmother's last sighting as February 18, 1991; records that he *"had not been reported missing until [officers] interviewed her on July 24, 1991."* [Category A, B, D]
2. **Document Title & Date:** Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint, Case No. `F-912542` (August 6, 1991); MPD Transmittal Letter to FBI (August 12, 1991).
3. **Page / Entry:** Complaint p. 1 (Count 05) & p. 5 (Entry 1); FBI Part 06, p. 11 (Entry #8).
4. **Direct URLs:** [Criminal Complaint PDF](https://townsquare.media/site/722/files/2023/10/attachment-Jeffrey-Dahmer-Criminal-Complaint.pdf) | [FBI Part 06](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2006)
5. **What It Establishes vs. Does Not Establish:**
   * *Establishes:* The grandmother's last physical contact was February 18, 1991; no formal police report existed prior to July 24, 1991; the legal charge was anchored to February 18.
   * *Does Not Establish:* Does not prove death occurred on February 18; does not support the MJS retrospective date of March 7, 1991.
6. **Conflict:** Direct conflict between the grandmother's sworn statement (Feb 18) and the retrospective account (March 7).

### B. Ernest Miller
1. **Official Record Wording:**
   * *Complaint p. 4, Entry 1:* Sworn statement of aunt Vivian Miller that Ernest visited from Chicago for Labor Day and *"left her home during the early morning hours of September 3rd, 1990 and she has not seen him or heard from him since."* [Category A]
   * *FBI Part 06, p. 12:* Confirms he was *"last seen leaving a friend's home at 1:30 A.M. on September 3, 1990, and was reported missing to the Milwaukee Police Department by relatives on September 4, 1990."* [Category A, B]

2. **Document Title & Date:** Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint (August 6, 1991); MPD Transmittal Letter (August 12, 1991).
3. **Page / Entry:** Complaint p. 1 (Count 03) & p. 4 (Entry 1); FBI Part 06, p. 12 (Entry #11).
4. **Direct URLs:** [Criminal Complaint PDF](https://townsquare.media/site/722/files/2023/10/attachment-Jeffrey-Dahmer-Criminal-Complaint.pdf) | [FBI Part 06](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2006)
5. **What It Establishes vs. Does Not Establish:**
   * *Establishes:* Miller left his aunt's home in the early morning (1:30 A.M.) of September 3, 1990; relatives reported him missing on September 4.

### C. Jeremiah Weinberger
1. **Official Record Wording:**
   * *FBI Part 06, p. 9:* Records that Weinberger *"was reported missing by his father on July 6, 1991 to the Chicago Police Department."* [Category B]
   * *Complaint p. 7, Entry 1:* Dahmer confession states he met Weinberger in Chicago *"on or about July 5th, 1991,"* travelled by bus to Milwaukee, and *"this man stayed with him for two days... on the second day the man indicated that he wanted to leave... so he gave him a drink with a sleeping potion... and strangled him manually."* [Category C]
   * *Complaint p. 2, Count 10:* Formally charges murder occurring *"on or about July 7, 1991."* [Category C]
   * *7th Circuit Opinion (105 F.3d at 1184):* Shorthand background states Dahmer murdered Weinberger *"on or about July 6, 1991."* [Category E]
2. **Document Title & Date:** Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint (August 6, 1991); FBI Part 06 (August 12, 1991); *Weinberger v. Wisconsin* (7th Cir. 1997).
3. **Page / Entry:** Complaint p. 2 (Count 10) & p. 7 (Entry 1); FBI Part 06, p. 9 (Entry #3); 105 F.3d at 1184.
4. **Direct URLs:** [Criminal Complaint PDF](https://townsquare.media/site/722/files/2023/10/attachment-Jeffrey-Dahmer-Criminal-Complaint.pdf) | [FBI Part 06](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2006) | [Justia 7th Circuit Opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/105/1182/598676/)
5. **What It Establishes vs. Does Not Establish:**
   * *Establishes:* Weinberger was reported missing in Chicago on July 6; Dahmer alleged a two-day stay starting July 5; the state complaint formally charged murder on or about July 7.
   * *Does Not Establish:* Does not establish July 6 as a definitive judicial finding of death.
6. **Conflict:** The federal court opinion’s shorthand ("on or about July 6") conflicts with the state complaint charge ("on or about July 7").
