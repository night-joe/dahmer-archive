## Editorial & Methodological Standard

1. **Human-Centered Approach:** This table focuses on the lives, identities, relationships, and aspirations of the victims. Forensic and sensationalized murder details are strictly excluded.
2. **Chronological Structure:** Entries are arranged chronologically by reported last-known sighting rather than by any perceived hierarchy.
3. **Absence of "Date of Death":** In accordance with case evidence standards, "Date of death" is not used as a table field. Medical timestamps were not established for decomposed or destroyed remains; dates represent reported last-known sightings.
4. **Discrepancy Transparency:** Documented age or date conflicts between historical media accounts (e.g., the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective) and official government archives (FBI FOIA files, Milwaukee County court complaints) are retained with asterisks (`*`) and itemized in the audit notes below.
5. **Claim-Level Sourcing:** Citations reference the approved local Victims Bibliography (`[1]`–`[17]`).

## Methodological Framework & Audit Notes

### 1. Distinction of Record Dates
The dates presented above reflect the **reported last known date alive** recorded in primary investigative files and contemporaneous reporting. In case research, the following distinct milestones must not be conflated:
1. **Last Known Date:** The final confirmed date family members, friends, or witnesses saw or spoke with the victim alive.
2. **Missing Person Report Date:** The date local law enforcement formally accepted a missing-person report (often days or weeks after the disappearance).
3. **Alleged Offense Date:** The approximate date of homicide specified in the Milwaukee County District Attorney's charging complaints, largely derived from Dahmer's post-arrest interrogations.
4. **Forensic Identification Date:** The date the Milwaukee County Medical Examiner or FBI Laboratory conclusively established identity via latent fingerprints, odontological analysis, or forensic anthropology.

### 2. Itemized Victim Audit Notes

* **Steven Hicks:**
  * *Last Known Date:* The June 18, 1978 date is derived from the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1]. FBI Vault records (Part 01, pp. 24–25) record Hicks as a possible Ohio victim and document a June 24, 1978 Ohio missing-person report; the June 24 missing report is a distinct administrative event and must not be used to substantiate the June 18 sighting.
  * *Birth Date Discrepancy:* FBI Part 01 lists DOB as June 27, 1959, whereas other internal summaries list June 22, 1959. Both dates establish his age as 18 on June 18, 1978.
  * *Forensic Recovery:* Hicks's remains were recovered in Bath Township, Ohio, in August 1991 and identified in September 1991 [4, 17].

* **Steven Tuomi:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`28*`):* The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1] and July 1991 press accounts report his age as 28. However, FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 43 records detectives' belief that Tuomi's DOB was December 19, 1962, which would imply age 24 on September 15, 1987. This discrepancy may reflect differing age baselines (age at disappearance vs. age at 1991 identification).
  * *Death Chronology & Legal Status:* Dahmer's confession stated the homicide occurred at the Ambassador Hotel "somewhere around 1986 or 1987" [2 (Part 03, PDF p. 10)]. Because no physical remains were recovered, prosecutors lacked independent corpus delicti; Tuomi was the only Milwaukee victim for whom no formal criminal charge was filed [15, 16].

* **James "Jamie" Doxtator:**
  * *Last Known Date:* The January 16, 1988 date is established by the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1]. Contemporary reporting (UPI, July 26, 1991 [14]) references January 1988 without pinpointing the exact calendar day.
  * *Investigative Descriptors:* FBI Part 01, PDF p. 43 documents detectives' notes on "Jamie E. Doxator" with DOB March 1, 1973 (confirming age 14). The FBI notes describe him as "a Hispanic male," whereas family records and news retrospectives confirm Stockbridge and Oneida ancestry [1].

* **Richard Guerrero:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`25*`):* Three distinct ages appear across sources: MJS reports age 25 [1]; UPI (July 30, 1991 [11]) reports age 23 at disappearance; FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 13 records DOB December 12, 1965, implying age 22 at the time of the March 29, 1988 missing report.
  * *Disappearance Period:* Guerrero was last seen in late March 1988; the formal missing-person report was accepted March 29, 1988 [2].

* **Anthony Sears:**
  * *Age Verification:* Corrected from earlier working drafts that listed age 26. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 24 records DOB January 28, 1965, confirming age 24 on March 25, 1989.
  * *Timeline Dissonance:* MJS retrospective records last seen March 25, 1989 [1]. UPI reports he met Dahmer on March 26 and planned to visit family for Easter on March 27. The criminal complaint alleges homicide on or about March 26 [3]. Easter Sunday was March 26, 1989, reflecting minor date confusion in contemporaneous press accounts.

* **Raymond "Ricky Beeks" Smith:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`33*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 33 [1]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 25 records DOB August 10, 1957, which implies age 32 on the May 29, 1990 last-seen date.
  * *Naming Convention:* The entry combines his legal name and commonly used moniker ("Ricky Beeks") to preserve family recognition and public record consistency.

* **Edward "Eddie" Smith:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`28*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 28 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 13 lists birth records that indicate age 27 at disappearance.
  * *Last Known Date:* Reported missing in June 1990 [1, 11]. Shortly after, his sister Carolyn received an anonymous phone call stating Eddie was dead [1, 11].

* **Ernest Miller:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`24*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 24 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 12 records DOB May 5, 1967, implying age 23 on September 2, 1990.
  * *Chronology Separation:* MJS and UPI [1, 11] report last seen September 2, 1990. The Milwaukee County complaint [3] records aunt Vivian Miller's statement that Ernest left home around 1:30 a.m. on September 3, was reported missing September 4, and alleges homicide on or about September 3.

* **David Thomas:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 23. Disappeared September 24, 1990; missing person report filed by partner Chandra Beanland in September 1990 [1, 11].

* **Curtis Straughter:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`17–18*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 18 [1]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 25 records DOB April 16, 1973, confirming he was 17 on the February 18, 1991 sighting. UPI [10] reports him as 18 at post-arrest identification.
  * *Last Sighting Conflict:* MJS gives last seen March 7, 1991 [1]. FBI Part 06, PDF p. 11 and the criminal complaint [3] record his grandmother's last sighting as February 18, 1991. The formal missing report was filed July 24, 1991 [2].

* **Errol Lindsey:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 19. Last seen April 7, 1991 [1]. Family impact statement at sentencing delivered by sister Rita Isbell [8].

* **Anthony "Tony" Hughes:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 31. Last seen May 24, 1991 [1]. Sourced life profile emphasizes his active communication skills, bilingual signing/lip-reading, and close family ties [1, 8].

* **Konerak Sinthasomphone:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 14. Reported missing May 1991; police encounter occurred May 27, 1991 [1, 7, 10].
  * *Contextual Rule:* Family arrival is documented as 1980 [1, 6]. Deep analysis of the May 27 police failure is maintained in the Systemic Failures section to avoid reducing his biographical profile purely to institutional negligence.

* **Matt Turner:**
  * *Identity & Age Verification:* Age 20. Last seen June 30, 1991 [1, 12]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 confirms alias Donald Montrell and DOB July 3, 1970.

* **Jeremiah Weinberger:**
  * *Record Verification:* Age 23. Last seen July 6, 1991 [1, 12]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 confirms DOB September 29, 1967.

* **Oliver Lacy:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`23*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 23 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 records DOB June 23, 1967, implying age 24 on July 12, 1991. An earlier FBI entry in Part 01 implies 1968 (age 23).
  * *Chronology Separation:* Last seen July 12, 1991 [1, 12]; reported missing July 15, 1991; identified via fingerprints July 24, 1991 [2].

* **Joseph Bradehoft:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 25. Last seen July 16, 1991 [1, 12]. Final known victim; encountered near Marquette University campus [1, 9].
