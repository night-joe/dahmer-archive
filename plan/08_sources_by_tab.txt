PURPOSE
-------
Definitive single working repository of research sources, primary archival records,
judicial dockets, and editorial standards for every webapp tab and the Sources page.

HOW THIS FILE IS ORGANIZED
--------------------------
- One heading per tab (matching the header navigation).
- Tabs with multiple articles have sub-headings per article.
- Entries are numbered within each tab for easy manual reference.
- The same source may appear under several tabs. That is intentional;
  pick the copy that fits the section you are working on.
- Section E of the IMPACT section is synchronized with the locked content plan
  (07_impact_and_legacy_content_plan_v02_final_locked.txt) and its 52 verified
  sources (08_impact_final_bibliography_v01.txt, [SRC-01]-[SRC-52]).
- The final Methodology, Research Standards, and Primary Repositories section
  governs /sources.html.

CONVENTIONS / CAUTIONS
----------------------
- [PRIMARY]      = official record, court document, government/agency file
- [CONTEMPORARY] = reporting published at the time of events (1991–1995)
- [SCHOLARLY]    = peer-reviewed or academic reference work
- [SECONDARY]    = later books/reporting/documentaries — good for context,
                   verify factual claims against primary sources
- [TERTIARY / INDEX ONLY] = Wikipedia, A&E, Biography.com, Today.com etc.
                   Use only to locate better sources; do not cite as evidence.
- Some archives are paywalled (Washington Post, NYT, jsonline). Use library
  access, publisher archives, or archive.today copies where needed.
- UPI Archives and WaPo/NYT archives are searchable; individual articles are
  linked where already located.
- All URLs were located via web search on 2026-10-06. Re-verify before final
  citation in the Sources phase.

   Official criminal complaint, preliminary hearing minutes, 15 homicide charging schedules,

   URL: https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer

   Television news broadcast collections (WTMJ-TV, WITI-TV, WISN-TV), newspaper morgues,
   and contemporaneous civic records.
4. Milwaukee Public Library Central Branch Periodical Archives.
   Complete microfilm holdings of The Milwaukee Journal and The Milwaukee Sentinel
   (July 1991 – December 1994).
5. U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin (Milwaukee) & Western
   District of Wisconsin (Madison) Federal Records.
   Dockets and opinions for Estate of Sinthasomphone, Balcerzak v. Milwaukee, and
   Weinberger v. Wisconsin.
6. U.S. National Archives and Records Administration (NARA), Chicago Regional Facility.
   Historical federal records and regional civil rights compliance documentation.

B. EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY & RESEARCH STANDARDS
1. Multi-Source Verification Rule:
   Every factual claim concerning dates, victim ages, crime locations, and police
   interactions is cross-referenced across at least two independent primary records
   or contemporary court documents.
2. Discrepancy Transparency Principle:
   Where primary records conflict (such as the timeline of the Steven Tuomi homicide
   or minor age variations in 1991 newspapers), the archive explicitly states the
   divergence rather than enforcing artificial precision.
3. Non-Graphic Representation & Human Dignity Standard:
   Strict omission of graphic crime-scene photographs or sensationalized autopsy imagery,
   prioritizing the biographical preservation and human dignity of the victims.
4. Fact vs. Interpretation Distinction:
   Rigid separation between established legal/physical facts (court records, ballistics,
   pathology) and speculative or theoretical models (forensic psychiatry, psychiatric
   diagnoses, psychological theories).
