## Verified Findings

### 1. Executive Summary of Primary Verification
A comprehensive, physical page-by-page visual inspection of the three standalone FBI Vault releases locally available (`Dahmer Part 01.pdf` [68 pp.], `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf` [60 pp.], and `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 06 of 19.pdf` [74 pp.]) was executed to verify every citation, claim-to-locator mapping, and date/DOB anchor in Table 1: The Victims.

The audit confirms the following primary facts:
1. **100% Primary DOB Anchoring Verified:** All 17 victims listed in Table 1 possess documented birth dates in official investigative records within the FBI Vault releases.
2. **David Courtney Thomas Primary DOB Independently Confirmed:** The investigative birth date of December 21, 1967 (`DOB: 12/21/67`) is recorded verbatim in `Dahmer Part 01.pdf` (PDF Page 39) and `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 06 of 19.pdf` (PDF Page 13). His calculated age on his disappearance date (September 24, 1990) is **22 years, 9 months, 3 days** (displayed as `22*`). The complete retirement of the provisional dagger symbol (`†`) in Table 1 is forensically confirmed and verified.
3. **Standalone PDF Pagination Established:** Legacy references to multi-part compilation scans or unmapped page numbering (such as "PDF Page 170" or "PDF Page 202") have been systematically traced and mapped to the exact 1-based physical pages of the standalone FBI Vault releases.
4. **Survivor Tracy Edwards Context Verified:** The survival of Tracy Edwards and the circumstances of Dahmer's arrest on July 22, 1991, are documented across three separate FBI transmissions in Part 01 (PDF Pages 4, 46, and 63), Dahmer's Behavioral Science Unit FD-302 interview in Part 03 (PDF Pages 11–12), and contemporary news clippings preserved in Part 03 (PDF Page 55).
5. **Rigorous Separation of Legal and Chronological Milestones:** The primary records clearly separate five distinct categories of events:
   - *Category A: Last-Known Sighting / Alive Contact* (eyewitness or family statement)
   - *Category B: Law Enforcement Missing-Person Report* (formal administrative filing date)
   - *Category C: Alleged Offense Date* (charging document count or confession narrative)
   - *Category D: Forensic Identification / Notification Date* (AFIS fingerprints, dental records, or photo confirmation)
   - *Category E: Investigative Descriptor / Detective Belief* (non-civil administrative data)

## Proposed Corrections

Based on direct visual examination of the primary FBI files and cross-referencing against existing project documentation, the following six specific corrections are proposed:

### 1. Permanent Deprecation and Replacement of Legacy Locators
* **Defect Identified:** Working drafts prior to version 4.0 (`Dahmer_Victims_Evidence_Anchor_and_Date_Audit_Report_corrected.md`, earlier citation trackers) cited non-existent physical pages in Part 01, such as "PDF Page 170" (for a master victim DOB table), "PDF Page 202" (for the Ambassador Hotel interrogation transcript), "PDF Page 106–107", and "PDF Page 167".
* **Physical Fact:** The standalone FBI Vault PDF `Dahmer Part 01.pdf` contains exactly **68 pages**, and `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf` contains exactly **60 pages**. Therefore, page numbers $\ge 69$ cannot exist in standalone Part 01.
* **Proposed Correction:** Replace all legacy citations across all audit reports and bibliographies with the verified 1-based standalone physical PDF pages:
  - Tuomi Ambassador Hotel confession: Map to **Part 03, PDF Page 10** (FD-302 Page 8).
  - Tuomi detective belief & Cass St address: Map to **Part 01, PDF Page 43 and Page 53**.
  - Hicks Cleveland Division investigation & last-seen date: Map to **Part 03, PDF Pages 51–52**.
  - Ten-victim identification teletype list: Map to **Part 01, PDF Pages 24–25**.
  - Hicks, Thomas, Miller, E. Smith, Guerrero teletype list: Map to **Part 01, PDF Pages 39–40**.
  - Five-victim unrecovered remains / NCIC search memo: Map to **Part 01, PDF Page 60**.
  - Milwaukee Police Department 14-victim transmittal list: Map to **Part 06, PDF Pages 9–13**.

### 2. Elimination of the Phantom Name "Townsend"
* **Defect Identified:** The dispatch instructions for Agent A listed the following parenthetical sequence: *"all 17 victims: Hicks, Tuomi, Guerrero, Sears, Thomas, Doxtator, Smith, Townsend, Lacy, Hughes, Konerak Sinthasomphone, Straughter, Weinberger, Turner, Bradehoft, plus Edwards survival context"*.
* **Physical Fact:** A complete audit of the primary FBI Vault files, the Milwaukee County Criminal Complaints, and the 17-victim Table 1 register confirms that **no person named Townsend was ever a victim of Jeffrey Dahmer**, nor was anyone named Townsend charged, identified, or suspected. The 17 victims murdered by Dahmer are: Hicks, Tuomi, Doxtator, Guerrero, Sears, Raymond Smith, Edward Smith, Miller, Thomas, Straughter, Lindsey, Hughes, Sinthasomphone, Turner, Weinberger, Lacy, and Bradehoft. In the prompt's sequence, Ernest Miller and Errol Lindsey were inadvertently omitted, while "Townsend" was erroneously inserted (likely a clerical error or phonetic confusion with Coventry Township, OH, where Hicks resided, or Townsend Street in Milwaukee).
* **Proposed Correction:** Explicitly record in project errata that "Townsend" is a phantom reference with zero basis in the historical or primary record, and maintain the authoritative 17-victim roster as established in `table_1_the_victims_v04.md`.

### 3. Resolution of the Jeremiah Weinberger "September 28" Variant
* **Defect Identified:** Earlier project drafts recorded three distinct DOB variants for Jeremiah Weinberger: September 25, September 28, and September 29, 1967.
* **Physical Fact:** Direct inspection of Part 06, PDF Page 9, Item #3 reveals that the typed text clearly reads: `dob: 9-29-67`. No third standalone document in the FBI Vault contains the date September 28. The "September 28" entry was a clerical OCR artifact where the typed numeral `9` was misread as an `8`.
* **Proposed Correction:** Update all audit documentation to state that primary FBI Vault records document exactly two DOB variants: **September 25, 1967** (Part 01, p. 25) and **September 29, 1967** (Part 06, p. 9). Remove all references to "September 28" as an authentic document variant.

### 4. Correction of the Matt Turner Part 01 DOB Attribution
* **Defect Identified:** Some early evidence notes claimed that Part 01, PDF Page 25 supported a date of birth for Matt Turner.
* **Physical Fact:** Verbatim inspection of Part 01, PDF Page 25, Item 8 shows: `"8) (X)MATT TURNER, AKA DONALD MONTRELL(X). POSITIVELY IDENTIFIED THROUGH CHICAGO AFIS SYSTEM BASED ON FINGERPRINTS."` The entry contains **no date of birth**. The sole primary DOB anchor for Matt Turner in the standalone FBI Vault releases is located in Part 06, PDF Page 9, Item #2 (`dob: 7-3-70`).
* **Proposed Correction:** Cite Part 01, PDF Page 25 strictly for his legal name, alias Donald Montrell, and Chicago AFIS fingerprint identification. Cite Part 06, PDF Page 9 as the exclusive primary source for his birth date (`July 3, 1970`).

### 5. Correction of the Edward W. Smith Dental Identification Claim
* **Defect Identified:** Part 01, PDF Page 39 (teletype DE FBIMW 0003, dated August 1, 1991) states: `"4) (X)EDWARD W. SMITH, BLACK MALE, DOB 8/2/62, 3606 NORTH 11TH STREET, MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN(X) IDENTIFIED THROUGH DENTAL RECORDS"`.
* **Physical Fact:** This early teletype transmission was an investigative error. Subsequent formal reports (Part 06, PDF Page 13 and Part 01, PDF Page 60) explicitly clarify that **no physical remains of Edward W. Smith were ever recovered**, that he was identified based upon Dahmer's confession and missing-person records, and that he was designated as victim #12 without remains.
* **Proposed Correction:** Note in the audit register that Part 01 p. 39's mention of "IDENTIFIED THROUGH DENTAL RECORDS" was an erroneous preliminary field statement, superseded by Part 06 p. 13 and Part 01 p. 60.

### 6. Formal Retirement of the Provisional Dagger (`†`) Symbol
* **Defect Identified:** In versions 1.0 through 3.0 of Table 1, David Thomas was marked with a provisional dagger (`23†`) because early auditors had not located a primary investigative DOB anchor.
* **Physical Fact:** Physical inspection of Part 01, PDF Page 39 and Part 06, PDF Page 13 located his official investigative record: `David C. Thomas, B/M, DOB: 12/21/67`. On his disappearance date of September 24, 1990, he was **22 years, 9 months, 3 days old**.
* **Proposed Correction:** Maintain David Thomas as **`22*`** in Table 1 (conforming to the age-at-disappearance standard), and confirm the total retirement of the dagger (`†`) symbol across all project files.

## Unresolved Discrepancies

### 1. Internal FBI Vault Document Contradictions

The following four discrepancies represent direct, irreconcilable contradictions internal to the FBI Vault documents themselves. Because these documents are official law enforcement transmissions created by different field offices or administrative units, neither variant can be dismissed as "unofficial," and civil vital statistics birth certificates would be required for absolute legal determination:

1. **Oliver Lacy — Birth Year 1968 vs. 1967 (Age 23 vs. 24):**
   - *Variant A (June 23, 1968):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 8 (teletype DE FBIMW 0001: `"AGE 23, DOB: 6 23/68"`), Part 01, PDF Page 24 (teletype DE FBIMW 0008: `"DOB 6/23/68"`), and Part 01, PDF Page 63 (airtel narrative: `"age 23, date of birth June 23, 1968"`). Under this date, his age on his disappearance date (July 12, 1991) was **23 years, 19 days**.
   - *Variant B (June 23, 1967):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 62 (Form FD-705 Airtel: `"DOB: 6/23/67"`) and Part 06, PDF Page 9 (MPD Transmittal Item #1: `"dob: 6-23-67"`). Under this date, his age on his disappearance date was **24 years, 19 days**.
   - *Status:* **Unresolved primary source contradiction.** Table 1 correctly displays `23*` (corroborated by the three Part 01 records and contemporary press reporting) while transparently citing the 1967 variant in the audit notes.

2. **Jeremiah Weinberger — Birth Day September 25 vs. September 29, 1967:**
   - *Variant A (September 25, 1967):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 25 (teletype DE FBIMW 0008: `"DOB 9/25/67"`). Under this date, his age on July 6, 1991 was **23 years, 9 months, 11 days**.
   - *Variant B (September 29, 1967):* Recorded in Part 06, PDF Page 9 (MPD Transmittal Item #3: `"dob: 9-29-67"`). Under this date, his age on July 6, 1991 was **23 years, 9 months, 7 days**.
   - *Status:* **Unresolved day-level contradiction.** Because both primary variants yield mathematically identical completed years (**Age 23**), displayed age `23*` in Table 1 is fully sound; the asterisk properly flags the document-level discrepancy.

3. **Steven Mark Hicks — Birth Day June 22 vs. June 27, 1959:**
   - *Variant A (June 22, 1959):* Recorded in Part 03, PDF Page 51 (Cleveland Division Report `CV 7-MW-26057`: `"date of birth June 22, 1959"`) and Part 01, PDF Page 39 (teletype DE FBIMW 0003: `"DOB 6/22/59"`).
   - *Variant B (June 27, 1959):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 25 (teletype DE FBIMW 0008: `"DOB 6/27/59"`).
   - *Status:* **Unresolved day-level contradiction.** On his disappearance date of June 18, 1978, Hicks was **18 years old** under both dates (four days before his 19th birthday under June 22; nine days under June 27). Displayed age `18*` in Table 1 correctly preserves this conflict.

4. **Richard Guerrero — Birth Day December 12 vs. December 6, 1965:**
   - *Variant A (December 12, 1965):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 60 (teletype DE FBIMW 0011: `"DOB: 12/12/65"`) and Part 06, PDF Page 13 (MPD Transmittal Item #14: `"DOB: 12/12/65"`).
   - *Variant B (December 6, 1965):* Recorded in Part 01, PDF Page 40 (teletype DE FBIMW 0003: `"DOB 12/6/65"`).
   - *Status:* **Unresolved minor day-level contradiction.** Both dates yield **Age 22** during his March 1988 disappearance. Displayed age `22*` is forensically supported.

### 3. Chronological and Legal Event Discrepancies

The audit identified significant date discrepancies where distinct chronological events must not be conflated:

1. **Curtis Straughter — February 18 vs. March 7, 1991 Sighting:**
   - *Primary Evidence:* Grandmother Katherine Straughter's sworn statement in the criminal complaint (p. 5) and MPD record (Part 06, p. 11) establishes that she last saw her grandson at home on **February 18, 1991**. Count 05 formally alleges homicide occurring *"on or about February 18, 1991."*
   - *Press Evidence:* The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1] gives **March 7, 1991** as his disappearance date.
   - *Status:* **Unresolved last-seen date discrepancy.** Table 1 appropriately presents both dates (`February 18, 1991 [complaint/FBI]; March 7, 1991 [MJS]`) and maintains age `17*` (as he was 17 on both dates).

2. **Ernest Miller — September 2 vs. September 3, 1990 Departure:**
   - *Primary Evidence:* Aunt Vivian Miller's sworn statement in the criminal complaint (p. 4) and MPD record (Part 06, p. 12) establishes that Miller was *"last seen leaving a friend's home at 1:30 A.M., on September 3, 1990"* (Labor Day morning).
   - *Press Evidence:* MJS [1] and UPI reports give **September 2, 1990** (the preceding Sunday evening of Labor Day weekend).
   - *Status:* Minor date-boundary variance between late evening September 2 and early morning (1:30 A.M.) September 3. Table 1 accurately records `September 2–3, 1990`.

3. **Anthony Sears — March 25/26 Chronology and Easter Date Error:**
   - *Primary Evidence:* Part 06, p. 12 records Sears was last seen at home on **March 25, 1989 at 9:30 A.M.** Sworn complaint narrative (p. 3) records friend Jeffrey Connor's statement that they went to a tavern on the evening of March 25, and Sears met Dahmer around closing time (early morning March 26). Count 01 alleges homicide *"on or about March 26, 1989."* Missing report filed March 29, 1989.
   - *Press Evidence:* UPI (08/16/1991 [14]) states that police placed the meeting on the night of March 26 and notes Sears told his mother he would be home for *"Easter on March 27."*
   - *Calendar Fact:* Western Easter Sunday in 1989 fell on **March 26, 1989**, not March 27. The UPI report represents a journalistic confusion with Easter Monday.
   - *Status:* Reconciled timeline layers preserved in Table 1 notes.

4. **James Doxtator — January 16/18, 1988 vs. "Spring of 1988":**
   - *Primary Evidence:* Part 01, p. 43 records detectives' notes of an alleged killing on **01/18/1988**. In contrast, Part 01, p. 60 states that Doxtator disappeared in the **"spring of 1988."**
   - *Press Evidence:* MJS [1] reports disappearance date of **January 16, 1988**.
   - *Status:* Unresolved. If disappearance occurred in January 1988, he was 14; if in spring 1988 after his March 1 birthday, he was 15. Table 1 retains `14*` to preserve this ambiguity.

5. **Steven Tuomi — Approximate Confession vs. Physical Charging Standard:**
   - *Primary Evidence:* Part 03, p. 10 records Dahmer's statement placing the homicide at the Ambassador Hotel *"somewhere around 1986 or 1987."* Part 06, pp. 3 and 15 documents an apartment evidence transmittal for crimes committed *"Between November 1987 and July 19, 1991."*
   - *Legal Fact:* District Attorney E. Michael McCann excluded Tuomi from the state criminal complaint because no physical remains were ever found, making corpus delicti impossible to establish in court (*The Washington Post*, Aug. 23, 1991).
   - *Status:* Unresolved exact date of death. Table 1 appropriately displays last-known date `September 15, 1987 [1]`.

6. **Oliver Lacy & Joseph Bradehoft — Disappearance Gaps Before Alleged Encounters:**
   - *Oliver Lacy:* Missing since July 12, 1991 (Part 06 p. 9); reported missing July 15; complaint alleges encounter on or about July 15. A documented 3-day disappearance gap exists.
   - *Joseph Bradehoft:* Last seen alive leaving for an interview July 16, 1991 (MJS/UPI); complaint alleges encounter on or about July 19. A documented 3-day gap exists. Earlier contacts (Minneapolis June 4; phone call July 4) are distinct baseline contacts.
   - *Status:* Preserved as distinct chronological milestones in Table 1.
