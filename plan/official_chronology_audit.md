### 1.2 Statutory Architecture: Wisconsin Criminal Code Revision (1988 / 1989)
A critical legal finding frequently overlooked in secondary literature and popular summaries concerns the formal classification of the fifteen Milwaukee County homicide counts. 

Effective **January 1, 1989**, the Wisconsin Legislature substantially revised the state's homicide statutes (1987 Wis. Act 399):
- **Pre-1989 Law (Wis. Stat. § 940.01 (1987)):** The capital-equivalent offense was styled **First-Degree Murder** ("whoever causes the death of another human being with intent to kill that person or another").
- **Post-1989 Law (Wis. Stat. § 940.01(1) (eff. Jan. 1, 1989)):** The offense was renamed and restyled as **First-Degree Intentional Homicide** ("whoever causes the death of another human being with intent to kill that person or another is guilty of a Class A felony").

Because Dahmer's Milwaukee killings spanned from 1987 to 1991, prosecutors could not charge all fifteen homicides under the same statutory label:
* **Two Counts of First-Degree Murder:** James Doxtator (alleged offense January 1988) and Richard Guerrero (alleged offense March 1988) were charged under pre-1989 Wis. Stat. § 940.01.
* **Thirteen Counts of First-Degree Intentional Homicide:** Anthony Sears (alleged offense March 1989) and all twelve subsequent victims killed between 1990 and 1991 were charged under post-1989 Wis. Stat. § 940.01(1).

Describing Dahmer's Milwaukee prosecution as "fifteen counts of first-degree intentional homicide" is legally erroneous. Wisconsin District Attorney E. Michael McCann specifically drafted the charges in conformity with the statutory version in effect at the time of each alleged killing.

### 1.4 Out-of-State Jurisdiction: Steven Hicks (Summit County, Ohio)
- **Jurisdictional Separation:** Because Steven Hicks was killed in Bath Township, Summit County, Ohio on June 18, 1978, the State of Wisconsin possessed no territorial subject-matter jurisdiction to charge Dahmer for his death.
- **Forensic Excavation & Recovery:** Summit County Sheriff's deputies and forensic anthropologists excavated the former Dahmer family residence in late July and August 1991, uncovering bone fragments and dental remains.
- **Indictment and Conviction:** Dahmer was indicted by a Summit County grand jury for Aggravated Murder (Ohio Rev. Code § 2903.01). On May 1, 1992, Dahmer was extradited to Akron, Ohio, entered a formal guilty plea before Judge James R. Williams in the Summit County Court of Common Pleas, and received his sixteenth consecutive life sentence.

### 1.5 Prosecutorial Non-Charging: Steven Tuomi and the *Corpus Delicti* Rule
- **The Confession:** Dahmer confessed to detectives that his second killing was Steven W. Tuomi at the Ambassador Hotel in Milwaukee "somewhere around 1986 or 1987," after which he placed Tuomi's body into a suitcase, transported it to his grandmother's home in West Allis, and dismembered and disposed of the remains in the trash.
- **The Legal Impediment:** Despite exhaustive searches, no physical remains, biological material, clothing, personal effects, or hotel registry records corroborating Tuomi's homicide were ever recovered.
- **The Corpus Delicti Standard:** Under Wisconsin law (*State v. Verhasselt*, *Bies v. State*), a defendant's extrajudicial confession alone is insufficient to support a criminal conviction or sustain a bindover at a preliminary hearing without independent corroborating evidence establishing that a crime was actually committed (*corpus delicti*).
- **DA McCann's Formal Ruling:** On August 22, 1991, District Attorney E. Michael McCann formally announced that no criminal charge would be filed regarding Steven Tuomi, stating that the prosecution would strictly limit charges to cases where probable cause could be legally sustained in court. Tuomi remains the only acknowledged Wisconsin victim for whom no criminal charges were brought.

### 1.7 Definition of the Four Mandatory Chronological Milestones
To eliminate chronological ambiguity, all records are audited against four strictly partitioned milestones:

1. **Milestone 1: Date of Last-Known Contact**  
   The final verifiable calendar date or documented time when the victim was seen alive, spoke by telephone, or had physical contact with family members, friends, coworkers, or witnesses.
2. **Milestone 2: Missing-Person Report Filing Date**  
   The official administrative date when a parent, spouse, relative, or partner formally filed a missing-person report with a law enforcement agency (MPD, Chicago PD, Madison PD, Greenville PD, Summit County Sheriff).
3. **Milestone 3: Alleged Offense Date**  
   The specific date or date range alleged by prosecutors in a sworn criminal complaint or information, or confessed by Dahmer to detectives.
4. **Milestone 4: Official Identification Date**  
   The formal calendar date and time when the Milwaukee County Medical Examiner, coroner, or police agency positively established the victim's identity (via fingerprint AFIS matching, postmortem dental records, polaroid photographic matching, or next-of-kin notification).

## Unresolved Discrepancies

The following discrepancies represent genuine conflicts or ambiguities between official records, contemporary press reporting, and retrospective accounts that cannot be definitively reconciled from the face of the documents and must remain transparently visible for human review:

### UD-01: Steven Tuomi — Contradictory Offense Date Ranges and HIATUS Record
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * FBI Part 03, PDF Page 10 (FD-302 Page 8): Dahmer stated his second murder was Steven Tuomi at the Ambassador Hotel *"somewhere around 1986 or 1987."*
  * FBI Part 06, PDF Pages 3 and 15 (MPD evidence transmittal letters): Transmits evidence for crimes committed *"Between November, 1987 and July 19, 1991."*
  * FBI Part 01, PDF Page 43 & 53: MPD detectives record that Tuomi was last seen on **September 15, 1987**.
  * Retrospective Press: Some later accounts cite November 20, 1987 without primary documentary corroboration.
- **Analysis:** Dahmer admitted passing out intoxicated and finding Tuomi dead beside him, but could not recall the exact month or year. MPD transmittals specify a November 1987 lower bound, but this is an administrative date range, not a verified time of death. Because no physical remains or hotel records were ever recovered, the exact date of Tuomi's death remains legally and forensically unresolvable.
- **Recommendation:** Do not assign a precise date of death. Maintain September 15, 1987 as the last-known sighting date and document Dahmer's confession ("around 1986 or 1987") and the MPD range ("Between November, 1987...") as unresolved investigative bounds.

### UD-02: James "Jamie" Doxtator — January 1988 vs. "Spring of 1988" Disappearance
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * Milwaukee Journal Sentinel Retrospective [1]: Disappeared January 16, 1988.
  * Contemporary Wire Reports (UPI Aug 16, 1991 [14]): Reports disappearance occurred in "January 1988."
  * FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 43: Detectives' teletype notes a suspected killing date of `1/18/88`.
  * FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 60 (DE FBIMW 0011, Aug 23, 1991): Records that Doxtator *"was 14 years of age when he disappeared in the spring of 1988."*
- **Analysis:** If Doxtator disappeared in January 1988 (as alleged in the complaint and MJS), he was 14 years, 10 months old (DOB March 1, 1973). If he disappeared in the "spring of 1988" after his March 1 birthday, he would have been 15 years old. The FBI's own document on page 60 introduces an unresolved internal conflict by describing him as "14 years of age when he disappeared in the spring of 1988."
- **Recommendation:** Display age as `14*` in Table 1; maintain January 1988 as the best-supported reported period; preserve the FBI Part 01 p. 60 "spring of 1988" entry in audit notes.

### UD-03: Curtis Straughter — February 18 Sworn Sighting vs. March 7 Press Sighting
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * Sworn Criminal Complaint p. 5, Entry 1: Grandmother Katherine Straughter swore under oath that she *"last saw her grandson on February 18th, 1991."*
  * FBI Part 06, PDF Page 11: Confirms grandmother last saw him at home on February 18, 1991.
  * Milwaukee County Criminal Complaint Count 05: Formally charges homicide occurring *"on or about February 18, 1991."*
  * Milwaukee Journal Sentinel Retrospective [1]: Reports last seen March 7, 1991.
- **Analysis:** Katherine Straughter's sworn eyewitness statement in the court complaint and police records is the highest grade of contemporary evidence, firmly anchoring the last physical contact to February 18, 1991. The March 7 date appears solely in later media retrospectives without an underlying police record anchor.
- **Recommendation:** Display `February 18, 1991` (grandmother's sworn statement) with the `March 7, 1991` press date qualified in Table 1; explain that both dates yield age 17 under his April 16, 1973 DOB, while post-arrest reporting of age 18 reflected his age at identification after his April 1991 birthday.

### UD-04: Richard Guerrero — Tripartite Age Discrepancy
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Page 13 & Part 01, PDF Page 60: Record official DOB as **December 12, 1965** (`12/12/65`). (Minor variant `12/6/65` on Part 01 p. 40).
  * UPI Contemporary Report (July 30, 1991 [11]): Explicitly states Guerrero was **23 years old** when he disappeared in March 1988.
  * Milwaukee Journal Sentinel Retrospective [1]: Reports age **25**.
- **Analysis:**
  * Born 12/12/1965 $\rightarrow$ Age in March 1988 (disappearance) was **22 years, 3 months**.
  * Age in July/August 1991 (arrest and identification) was **25 years, 7 months**.
  * The MJS retrospective age of 25 is mathematically consistent with his age at the time of Dahmer's arrest in 1991. However, UPI's explicit contemporary statement that he was 23 *at the time of his disappearance* directly contradicts the FBI-recorded birth date (under which he could not have been 23 until December 1988). This three-way divergence cannot be resolved without a certified civil birth certificate.
- **Recommendation:** Maintain displayed age as `22*` (DOB-calculated age at disappearance); preserve UPI's reported age 23 and MJS's reported age 25 in the audit notes as an unresolved source discrepancy.

### UD-05: Anthony Sears — Closing-Hour Encounter vs. Easter Calendar Discrepancy
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * FBI Part 06, PDF Page 12: Last seen at home March 25, 1989 at 9:30 A.M.
  * Complaint p. 3 (Witness Jeffrey Connor): Sears and Connor were at a bar on the evening of Saturday, March 25, 1989; after closing (early hours of Sunday, March 26), Sears met "Jeff" and departed.
  * Complaint Count 01: Alleges homicide occurred *"on or about March 26, 1989."*
  * UPI Contemporary Report (Aug. 16, 1991 [14]): Police stated meeting occurred on the night of March 26, and Sears told his mother he would be home for Easter on March 27.
  * U.S. Census Bureau Perpetual Calendar: In 1989, Western Easter Sunday fell on **March 26, 1989**, not March 27.
- **Analysis:** Connor's sworn narrative places the meeting at bar-closing time (early hours of Sunday, March 26), which aligns with Count 01 alleging homicide on or about March 26. UPI's reference to Easter on March 27 reflects journalistic confusion between Easter Sunday and Easter Monday.
- **Recommendation:** Maintain March 25, 1989 as the last-known sighting date (home and bar outing); document the March 26 early morning encounter and alleged offense date; document the Easter calendar error in audit notes.

### UD-06: Oliver Lacy — Internal FBI Birth-Year Conflict (1967 vs. 1968)
- **Primary Records in Conflict:**
  * FBI Part 01, PDF Page 8 (Teletype July 24, 1991): `DOB: 6 23/68, age 23`.
  * FBI Part 01, PDF Page 24 (Teletype July 26, 1991): `DOB 6/23/68`.
  * FBI Part 01, PDF Page 63 (Narrative summary): `date of birth June 23, 1968, age 23`.
  * FBI Part 01, PDF Page 62 (FD-705 Airtel, Aug 7, 1991): `DOB: 6/23/67`.
  * FBI Part 06, PDF Page 9 (MPD transmittal list): `dob: 6-23-67`.
- **Analysis:** This discrepancy is entirely internal to official law enforcement transmissions:
  * If born June 23, 1968: Age on July 12, 1991 was **23 years, 19 days**.
  * If born June 23, 1967: Age on July 12, 1991 was **24 years, 19 days**.
  Both MJS [1] and contemporary press (UPI July 24, 1991) report age 23. Only an official Illinois/Cook County birth certificate can conclusively determine the true year.
- **Recommendation:** Retain displayed age as `23*` in Table 1; cite all five standalone FBI PDF page locators in audit notes; record the birth-year conflict as an unresolved administrative discrepancy.

### UD-07: Unreported Missing Persons in Law Enforcement Records
