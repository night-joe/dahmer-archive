### 2. Childhood & Animal Remains Chronology: Fact vs. Retrospective Psychiatry
- **Hernia Operation (Early 1964):**
  - Shortly before his fourth birthday, Dahmer underwent surgery for bilateral inguinal hernias.
  - *Factual Evidence vs. Retrospective Attribution:* Lionel Dahmer noted in his memoir that following the surgery, Jeffrey appeared noticeably more subdued, withdrawn, and timid (*A Father's Story*, pp. 28–29).
  - *Editorial Mandate:* Do not present the surgery as a medical or psychological cause of his later psychopathology. Contemporary pediatric medicine and psychiatric evaluations (Dr. Park Dietz, Dr. Fred Berlin) confirm there is no empirical causal link between routine hernia repair and the development of paraphilic violent behavior.
- **Animal Remains, Chemical Bleaching & Scientific Curiosity (1968–1974):**
  - *Father's Role:* Around age 8 to 10 (~1968–1970), Lionel Dahmer crawled beneath the family's Bath Road home with Jeffrey to clean out small animal carcasses and chicken bones deposited by previous occupants. Lionel demonstrated to Jeffrey how to clean, bleach, and preserve the bones using household chemicals. Lionel viewed this strictly as an educational father-son activity introducing chemistry, biology, and comparative anatomy.
  - *Stepmother Shari Dahmer's Corroboration:* Preserved in FBI clippings, `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 55 (Associated Press / Charlotte Observer, July 26, 1991):
    `"Relatives of Dahmer, who is from Medina, Ohio, said they discovered a vat filled with bones and slime three years ago, but couldn't tell if the remains were human or animal... 'Jeffrey said it was an animal he found,' said his stepmother, Shari. Dahmer liked to use acid to scrape the meat off dead animals. He told his father, Lionel, he was doing experiments."`
  - *Roadkill Collecting (~Age 10–14):* Dahmer began collecting animal carcasses (squirrels, raccoons, dogs, cats, possums) killed by vehicles along roads in Bath Township. He took them to a small wooden shed behind the house, disarticulated the skeletons, stripped the flesh, and preserved the bones in glass jars.
  - *Crucial Factual Separation:* In contemporary childhood observation, this was seen by family and peers as an eccentric, macabre hobby focused on taxidermy, osteology, and chemistry.
- **Post-1991 Retrospective Psychiatric Disclosure of Fantasies:**
  - During post-arrest interrogations (Detectives Patrick Kennedy and Dennis Murphy, July 1991) and the August 3, 1992 FBI BSU interview, Dahmer disclosed for the first time that his interest in animal remains had become sexually eroticized during early adolescence.
  - *FBI Locator:* `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 3:
    `"DAHMER stated that he had a fascination with death and when he was around 14 he used to bring home road kill, and then dissect it, because he liked to look at the internal organs. DAHMER stated that he got sexually aroused when he would look at the internal organs. DAHMER stated that he never had sex with any animals."`
  - *Strict Attribution:* The internal sexual arousal and necrophilic component was an unobservable subjective internal state disclosed retrospectively by Dahmer in 1991–1992, NOT a documented childhood observation.
- **Rigorous Disconfirmation of the Macdonald Triad:**
  - The classic criminological triad (enuresis, pyromania, animal cruelty) does NOT apply to Jeffrey Dahmer.
  - *FBI Primary Locator 1:* `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 8 (FD-302, Page 6):
    `"DAHMER was asked by the interview agents if he had any fascination with fire, or if he was a bedwetter as a child, or any cruelty to animals. DAHMER stated that he has never had any fascination with fire, nor was he ever a bedwetter, and the only fascination he had with animals was the road kill that he brought home to dissect. DAHMER stated that he was never cruel to any animals."`
  - *FBI Primary Locator 2:* `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 4 (FD-302, Page 2):
    `"DAHMER was asked by the interviewing agents if he had ever killed an animal, and DAHMER stated that he intended to on one occasion, where he had found an Irish setter and brought it home and was going to kill it and then skin it and dissect, but when the dog looked at him with its eyes, DAHMER could not kill it, so he just let the dog go."`
  - *Forensic Psychiatric Consensus:* Dr. Park Dietz (trial testimony, February 1992) and Dr. Fred Berlin concurred that Dahmer's psychopathology was characterized by severe necrophilic attraction and fear of abandonment, requiring absolute docility and passivity, entirely distinct from sadistic animal torture.

### 3. Revere High School (Bath, OH) & Alcoholism Chronology
- **High School Career:** Attended **Revere High School** in Bath Township (Richfield, Ohio) from September 1974 until graduation in **June 1978**.
- **School Activities & Social Presence:**
  - Music & Sports: Played clarinet in the Revere High School marching and concert band; competed on the junior varsity tennis team.
  - "Doing a Dahmer" Pranks: Classmates, most notably John "Derf" Backderf (*My Friend Dahmer*, 2012), documented that Dahmer gained brief social recognition by staging bizarre, disruptive comedic performances in hallways, classrooms, and local businesses, mocking spastic tics, bleating like a sheep, and simulating epileptic seizures.
  - Yearbook Prank: In Spring 1978, Dahmer slipped uninvited into the official Revere High School National Honor Society group photograph; when discovered prior to publication, school staff blacked out his likeness from the printing plate in many copies.
- **Severe Escalating Alcohol Dependence:**
  - Onset: Began drinking alcohol (beer and hard liquor) regularly around age 14 (ninth grade).
  - Progression: By his junior and senior years (1976–1978), Dahmer was chronically dependent, smuggling liquor into school inside his coat, locker, or briefcase, and drinking continuously before, during, and after class.
  - School Response: While teachers and students frequently noticed the smell of alcohol on his breath and observed him inebriated in class, no formal disciplinary action, medical intervention, or substance counseling was ever initiated by school administration.
  - *FBI Locator:* `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 3:

## Unresolved Discrepancies

The evidentiary audit identified the following specific empirical and clerical discrepancies across primary records, court files, and secondary sources:

### 1. Basic Training Installation: Fort McClellan, AL vs. Fort Leonard Wood, MO
- **Primary FBI Record:** `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 23 (official FBI teletype to Director/Bonn, December 22, 1992):
  `"...ENTERED THE ARMY ON 1/12/79, HAVING BASIC TRAINING AT FORT MCCLELLAN, ALABAMA, AND ADVANCED INFANTRY TRAINING AT FORT SAM HOUSTON, IN 1979."`
- **Contrasting Account:** Lionel Dahmer, *A Father's Story* (1994), p. 77, and Brian Masters, *The Shrine of Jeffrey Dahmer* (1993), p. 48, state that Dahmer completed basic combat training at **Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri**, before transferring to Fort Sam Houston.
- **Forensic Assessment:** Fort McClellan in Anniston, Alabama was a major basic training base (as well as the Military Police and Chemical Corps school) during 1979; Fort Leonard Wood in the Missouri Ozarks was also a primary basic training center. Because both sources have high credibility (FBI teletype quoting military records in Milwaukee FBI possession vs. father's contemporaneous correspondence), this stands as an unresolved historical variance. Both locations should be documented with attribution.

### 2. Typographical & Organizational Errors in Military Unit Designations
- **FBI Transcripts:**
  - `Dahmer Part 01.pdf`, physical Page 29: `"HEADQUARTERS COMPANY, 268TH ARMOR DIVISION, SECOND BATALLION, 68TH ARMOR IN BAUMHOLDER, GERMANY."`
  - `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 23: `"SERVED IN THE 268 ARMOR DIVISION, SECOND BATTALION."`
- **U.S. Army Force Structure Reality:** The U.S. Army has never had a "268th Armor Division" in its order of battle. The unit stationed in Baumholder, West Germany was the **2nd Battalion, 68th Armor Regiment** (`2-68 Armor`), which was an organic component of the **8th Infantry Division (Mechanized)** ("Pathfinder Division"). The FBI teletype typist conflated the battalion designation (`2nd`) with the regiment number (`68th`) to invent "268th Armor Division".

### 3. Fort Sam Houston Training Description
- **FBI Part 03 p. 23 Text:** Calls Fort Sam Houston training `"ADVANCED INFANTRY TRAINING"`.
- **Factual Reality:** Fort Sam Houston in San Antonio, Texas is the historic home of the U.S. Army Medical Department (AMEDD), not the Infantry School (which is at Fort Benning, Georgia). Fort Sam Houston conducted Advanced Individual Training (AIT) for Medical Specialist MOS 91B. The teletype typist made an obvious transcription error by expanding the acronym "AIT" as "Advanced Infantry Training" rather than "Advanced Individual Training".

### 4. Ramada Inn Arrest Citation in Existing Timeline Plan
- **Existing Draft:** `05_timeline_content_plan_v04_final_locked.txt` (line 53 and line 130) cites `[1 (Pt 01 p. 27)]` for the October 1981 Bath Township Ramada Inn arrest.
- **Audit Discovery:** An exhaustive line-by-line inspection of physical pages 26 through 32 of `Dahmer Part 01.pdf` reveals that the Ramada Inn arrest is **100% absent** from Part 01. Physical page 28 lists only the 1982 State Fair Park arrest, the 1986 Milwaukee arrest, and the 1988 child exploitation arrest.
- **Resolution:** The Ramada Inn citation to the FBI Vault is a phantom locator. The true primary/secondary authorities are Bath Township Police Department arrest dockets and Lionel Dahmer (*A Father's Story*, 1994, pp. 88–90).

### 5. Steven Mark Hicks Date of Birth Variance
- **Summit County Sheriff Missing Person Report & Primary FBI Teletype:** `Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03 of 19.pdf`, physical Page 51, and `Dahmer Part 01.pdf`, physical Page 39, record DOB **June 22, 1959** (`6/22/59`).
- **FBI Confirmation Teletype DE FBIMW 0008:** `Dahmer Part 01.pdf`, physical Page 25, records DOB **June 27, 1959** (`6/27/59`).
- **Resolution:** The date June 22, 1959 is the primary anchor recorded on the official Summit County Sheriff's missing-person docket; June 27, 1959 is an internal FBI teletype clerical error.

### 6. McKinley Beach vs. Kinnickinnic River Incident Geography
- **Primary FBI Record:** `Dahmer Part 01.pdf`, physical Page 28, states:
  `"SUBJECT WAS ARRESTED ON 9/8/86 BY THE MILWAUKEE, WISCONSIN POLICE DEPARTMENT ON A CHARGE OF LEWD AND LASCIVIOUS BEHAVIOR AND WAS PLACED ON PROBATION FOR ONE YEAR AND FINED."`
- **Geographic Nuance:** Contemporary Milwaukee press and court records report the arrest took place in the vicinity of McKinley Beach / Veterans Park along Lake Michigan, resulting in a specific court order banning Dahmer from visiting McKinley Beach during his probation. However, certain local police dispatch entries and later retrospective summaries make reference to an encounter near the Kinnickinnic River corridor on the South Side. Both locations reflect the lakefront/riverway recreational areas of Milwaukee where the offense and arrest were processed.
