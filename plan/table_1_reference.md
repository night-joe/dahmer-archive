### Audit notes

- **Steven Hicks:** The June 18 date is attributed to the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective. The FBI's June 24 missing-person report describes a different event, so it must not be used as confirmation of the June 18 sighting.
- **Oliver Lacy:** The asterisk matters. Two FBI records give different birth years—1967 and 1968—so his age on July 12 could be 24 or 23, respectively. The profile's reported age is 23, but the discrepancy should remain visible until resolved. The July 12 last-known date, July 15 missing report and July 24 identification are separate events.
