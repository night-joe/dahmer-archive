### Age discrepancy and date distinction

- **Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective [1]:** reports Straughter as 18 and gives March 7, 1991 as the last-seen date.
- **FBI Vault Part 06 [2]:** gives his DOB as April 16, 1973 and says his grandmother last saw him at home on February 18, 1991. That makes him 17 on that date.
- **Contemporary UPI report [new source to add]:** “Three more mass murder victims identified” (July 26, 1991) lists Straughter as 18. URL: https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/07/26/Three-more-mass-murder-victims-identified/5880680500800/
- **Criminal complaint [3]:** alleges the offense occurred “on or about February 18, 1991.” An alleged offense date is not automatically the same as the last date a person was seen; the FBI summary separately records the grandmother’s last sighting on that date.

**Recommended audit handling:** Keep the plan’s marked age (`17–18*` or the already-proposed `17*` with explicit note) until the final display decision. State the record conflict in the note: MJS reports 18 and March 7; the FBI record gives DOB April 16, 1973 and records the grandmother’s last sighting on February 18. Do not silently change either the age or date.

### Citation actions

1. Keep **[1]** for the career, education plans, Gay Youth Milwaukee involvement, and the retrospective’s report of his mother’s later statement.
2. Use **[2]** for the DOB and last-sighting information, citing the specific FBI Vault Part 06 record rather than only the general Vault landing page.
3. Use **[3]** for the complaint’s alleged offense date, accurately labelled as an allegation (“on or about”), not as conclusive proof of the last-seen date.
4. Add the specific UPI July 26, 1991 identification report to the source register; do not cite the generic portal [14] for the age claim.
