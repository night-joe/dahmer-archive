## Editorial \& Methodological Standard

1. **Human-Centered Approach:** This table focuses on the lives, identities, relationships, and aspirations of the victims. Forensic and sensationalized murder details are excluded.
2. **Chronological Structure:** Entries are arranged chronologically by the best-supported reported last-known date or period, not by any perceived hierarchy.
3. **No Implied Date of Death:** The table does not use “Date of death.” A last-known sighting, missing-person report, alleged offense date, and forensic identification date are different events and must not be conflated.
4. **Age Convention:** The preferred age is age on the best-supported last-known date, calculated from a documented birth date where available. Following primary-source audit of FBI Vault records (Parts 01, 03, and 06), all 17 victims now have documented birth-date anchors in official investigative records. An asterisk (`\\\*`) indicates a documented birth-date or age-reference discrepancy (such as when contemporary press reported age at post-arrest identification rather than recalculating age at disappearance, where investigative records list conflicting birth dates, or if uncertainty about the best-supported last-known date affects the calculated age). A documented DOB that cleanly corroborates the displayed age requires no marker. The provisional dagger (`†`) symbol is retired in Version 4.0.
5. **Date Qualification:** Where the exact day is not independently corroborated or the record identifies only a disappearance/reporting period, use the broader period and explain the limitation. Do not present a reported disappearance period as a confirmed last sighting.
6. **Claim-Level Sourcing:** Citations use the authoritative Victims bibliography numbering `\\\[1]`–`\\\[18]`. Additional sources unique to this table are assigned new numbers `\\\[19]`–`\\\[27]`; existing bibliography numbers are not reused for different publications.
7. **Audit Transparency:** Conflicting ages, dates, and source descriptions remain visible in the audit notes rather than being silently reconciled.

## Methodological Framework \& Audit Notes

### 1\. Distinction of Record Dates

The dates presented above reflect the best-supported reported last-known sighting or contact where one is established. Evidence strength varies by entry: some exact dates are reported in a retrospective source, some are supported by contemporary reporting, and some entries show a broader disappearance period or a significant police encounter. The table labels these distinctions and does not imply that every date is independently confirmed by a primary investigative record. In case research, the following distinct milestones must not be conflated:

1. **Last Known Date:** The final reported date family members, friends, or witnesses saw or spoke with the victim alive; it may remain disputed or unconfirmed.
2. **Missing Person Report Date:** The date local law enforcement formally accepted a missing-person report (often days or weeks after the disappearance).
3. **Alleged Offense Date:** The approximate date of homicide specified in the Milwaukee County District Attorney's charging complaints, largely derived from Dahmer's post-arrest interrogations.
4. **Forensic Identification Date:** The date authorities established identity using methods such as fingerprint comparison, dental records, or forensic anthropology.

### 2\. Itemized Victim Audit Notes

* **Steven Hicks:**

  * *Last Known Date:* Supported by retrospective accounts \[1] and explicitly corroborated in FBI Vault Part 03, PDF Page 52 (field report `CV 7-MW-26057`, Page 2), which records: *"HICKS was last seen on June 18, 1978."* Part 01, PDF p. 60 documents a June 24, 1978 Ohio missing-person report; the June 24 missing report is a distinct administrative event and must not be confused with the June 18 sighting.
  * *Birth Date Records:* FBI Part 03, PDF p. 51 and Part 01, PDF p. 39 list DOB as June 22, 1959. FBI Part 01, PDF p. 25 lists June 27, 1959. Both dates establish his age as 18 on June 18, 1978 (four days before his 19th birthday under the June 22 record).
  * *Forensic Recovery:* Hicks's remains were recovered in Bath Township, Ohio, in August 1991 and identified in September 1991 \[4, 17].
* **Steven Tuomi:**

  * *Age Reference Difference (`24\\\*`):* The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective \[1] and 1991 press accounts report age 28, which reflects his presumed age at the time of the 1991 investigation. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 43 and Page 53 record detectives' belief that Tuomi's DOB was December 19, 1962, which establishes age 24 on September 15, 1987. The table displays `24\\\*` under the age-at-last-known-date convention; the retrospective reported age of 28 remains documented here.
  * *Death Chronology \& Legal Status:* Dahmer's confession in FBI Vault Part 03, PDF Page 10 (FD-302 Page 8) stated the homicide occurred at the Ambassador Hotel *"somewhere around 1986 or 1987."* Because no physical remains were ever located, District Attorney E. Michael McCann determined there was insufficient evidence to establish corpus delicti / probable cause in court; hence, no criminal charge was filed in Milwaukee County \[15, 16].
* **James "Jamie" Doxtator:**

  * *Last Known Date \& Age Ambiguity (`14\\\*`):* The public-facing table uses January 1988 \[1, 2]. The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective \[1] gives January 16. FBI Part 01, PDF Page 43 documents detectives' notes on "Jamie E. Doxator" with DOB March 1, 1973 (confirming age 14 in January 1988) and notes an alleged killing date of 1/18/88. In contrast, FBI Part 01, PDF Page 60 documents his disappearance as occurring in the "spring of 1988"; if the disappearance occurred after his March 1 birthday, he would have been 15. The table displays `14\\\*` to flag this unresolved date- and age-reference discrepancy.
  * *Investigative Descriptors:* FBI Part 01, PDF Page 60 describes him as an "Indian male," consistent with Stockbridge and Oneida ancestry \[1].
* **Richard Guerrero:**

  * *Age Reference Difference (`22\\\*`):* The Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective reports age 25 \[1], while UPI (July 30, 1991 \[11]) reports age 23 at disappearance. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Page 13 and Part 01, PDF Page 60 record a date of birth of December 12, 1965, which yields age 22 during the March 1988 disappearance period and age 25 in July 1991. The MJS age of 25 is consistent with his age in 1991; UPI's reported age of 23 at disappearance is inconsistent with the FBI-recorded birth date and remains unresolved. The table displays `22\\\*` under the age-at-last-known-period convention.
  * *Disappearance Period:* Guerrero disappeared in late March 1988; the formal missing-person report was accepted March 29, 1988 \[2 (Part 06, PDF p. 13)].
* **Anthony Sears:**

  * *Age Verification:* FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 24 records DOB January 28, 1965, confirming age 24 on March 25, 1989. Displayed as `24` without marker.
  * *Timeline Dissonance:* FBI Vault Part 06, PDF Page 12 records that Sears was last seen at home on March 25, 1989 at 9:30 A.M.; reported missing March 29. Criminal complaint witness Jeffrey Connor stated that he and Sears were at a tavern on the evening of March 25; after closing (early morning March 26), Sears met Dahmer and left. Count 01 alleges homicide on or about March 26 \[3]. UPI reports Sears told his mother he would be home for Easter on March 27 \[14]. Western Easter Sunday in 1989 was March 26, so the UPI phrasing reflects contemporary journalistic confusion with Easter Monday.
* **Raymond "Ricky Beeks" Smith:**

  * *Age Reference Difference (`32*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 33 [1], and UPI's August 6, 1991 report also lists him as 33 [13]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 25, and Part 06, PDF Page 11 [2] record DOB August 10, 1957, establishing age 32 on the May 29, 1990 last-seen date reported by MJS [1]. The reported age of 33 is consistent with reporting in 1991, not his age on the last-seen date. The table uses `32*`.
  * *Naming Convention:* Combines legal name and commonly used moniker ("Ricky Beeks") to preserve family recognition and public record consistency. Part 06 p. 11 notes he was not formally reported missing prior to Dahmer's arrest.
* **Edward "Eddie" Smith:**

  * *Age Reference Difference (`27*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 28 [1], and UPI's July 30, 1991 report also describes Eddie Smith as 28 [11]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 39, and Part 06, PDF Page 13 [2] record DOB August 2, 1962, establishing age 27 on the June 14, 1990 last-seen date reported by his sister [11]. He was 28 during the July 1991 identification and reporting period, shortly before his August 2 birthday. The table displays `27*`.
  * *Last Known Date:* UPI reports that Eddie's sister last saw him at her home on June 14, 1990 \[11]; formal missing-person report was accepted June 23, 1990 (Part 06, PDF p. 13).

  * *Age Reference Difference (`23*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 24 [1], and UPI's July 30, 1991 report also gives age 24 [11]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF Page 39, and Part 06, PDF Page 12 [2] record DOB May 5, 1967, establishing age 23 in September 1990. The reported age of 24 reflects his age during the July 1991 identification and reporting period, after his May 5 birthday. The table displays `23*`.
  * *Chronology Separation:* MJS and UPI \[1, 11] report last seen September 2, 1990 (the Sunday evening of Labor Day weekend). The criminal complaint \[3] and FBI Part 06, PDF p. 12 record aunt Vivian Miller's statement that Ernest left home around 1:30 A.M. on September 3, was reported missing September 4, and alleges homicide on or about September 3.

## Concluding Note on Version 4.0 Specification Changes

> \\\[!IMPORTANT]
> \\\*\\\*Audit Resolution \\\& Retirement of the Provisional Dagger Convention:\\\*\\\*  
> In prior working drafts (v01–v03), David Thomas was the sole victim whose age could not be independently recalculated from a documented primary record, necessitating the provisional dagger marker (`23†`). Physical inspection of the local primary source repositories (`assets/Dahmer Part 01.pdf`, PDF Page 39, and `assets/Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 06 of 19.pdf`, PDF Page 13) located his official investigative entry: `David C. Thomas, B/M, DOB: 12/21/67`.  
> Recalculating his age on his September 24, 1990 disappearance date yields \\\*\\\*22 years, 9 months\\\*\\\*, demonstrating that the historically reported age of 23 reflected his age at the time of Dahmer's arrest and identification in July 1991.  
> The reviewed records show a recurring, but not universal, pattern: some contemporary and retrospective reports give victims' ages at identification or during the 1991 investigation rather than their ages at the time of disappearance. Other discrepancies remain unexplained or conflict with the birth dates recorded in investigative files. Richard Guerrero is one example: UPI reported age 23 at disappearance, while the FBI-recorded birth date yields age 22 for March 1988. These differences are documented individually in the audit notes rather than treated as a single, universal explanation. This correction brings David Thomas into conformity with the table's age-at-last-known-date convention. As a result, 100% of the 17 victims are now anchored to primary investigative birth dates, and the provisional dagger (`†`) symbol is officially retired from Table 1.
