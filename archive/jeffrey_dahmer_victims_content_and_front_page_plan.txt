PROJECT PRINCIPLE
-----------------
The Victims section should shift attention away from Dahmer and toward the
people who were killed: who they were, what is publicly known about their
lives, and what can be responsibly reconstructed from the historical record.

Core principle:
"Where the record is thin, leave it thin rather than filling gaps with
speculation."

The page should not treat the victims as numbered case entries or as a
ranking of importance.

TABLE 1
-------
Columns currently planned:
- Name
- Age
- Background
- Who They Were

"Who They Were" is the important human-centered field.

Important data-handling rule:
- Ages and some dates can differ across sources.
- Do not silently choose a number when reliable sources conflict.
- Where a documented discrepancy matters, use a source note.
- Prefer "Last known date" where an exact date of death is not securely
  established.
- Steven Tuomi is a particularly important example of uncertainty because
  remains were not recovered and the exact circumstances/date are not fully
  established in the public record.

INDIVIDUAL PROFILE STYLE
------------------------
Profiles should be visually consistent while allowing different lengths.

Suggested pattern:
    NAME
    Age · Place / Background

    Background
    Biographical material

    Life
    Family, school/work, interests, aspirations, relationships, community

    Case context
    Brief, factual sentence connecting the person to the case

    Sources [1] [2]

The exact subheading pattern can be adjusted during content finalization.

Editorial rule:
- Biographical information should dominate.
- Details about the killing itself should be brief and non-graphic.
- Later media controversies should normally live in Impact / Media & Cultural
  Portrayal rather than in the victim biography.

CURRENT RESEARCH DRAFT — 17 VICTIMS
-----------------------------------
1. STEVEN HICKS, 18
   - Born Steven Mark Hicks.
   - Grew up in Coventry Township near Akron, Ohio.
   - Son of Richard Hicks, a quality-control analyst, and Martha Hicks, a
     homemaker.
   - Graduated from Coventry High School in 1978.
   - Described by people who knew him as kindhearted, compassionate, helpful,
     and open to making new friends.
   - Was hitchhiking to a rock concert when Dahmer offered him a ride.
   - Became Dahmer's first victim on June 18, 1978.
   - Verification needed for exact biographical wording and date.

2. STEVEN TUOMI, AROUND 24
   - Steven W. Tuomi.
   - Grew up in Ontonagon, Michigan.
   - Graduated from Ontonagon High School in 1981.
   - Later worked at a Milwaukee restaurant.
   - Last seen leaving work on September 15, 1987.
   - Classmates reportedly remembered him as quiet and artistic.
   - Family did not report him missing until December after noticing uncashed
     paychecks and an untouched apartment.
   - Dahmer admitted killing him.
   - No remains were recovered and he was not formally charged with the murder.
   - Has a memorial at Holy Family Cemetery in Ontonagon.
   - Exact age/date of death should be handled carefully.

3. JAMES "JAMIE" DOXTATOR, 14
   - Half Stockbridge and part Oneida.
   - Oldest of four children.
   - Enjoyed pool and bike rides.
   - Reportedly nearly six feet tall despite being 14.
   - Mother Debbie Vega later described him as vivacious and social.
   - Had recently left home, reportedly to get away from an abusive stepfather.
   - Dahmer met him at a Milwaukee bus stop in January 1988.
   - Verify tribal/background wording and family-source attribution carefully.

4. RICHARD GUERRERO, 25
   - Came from a family of Mexican descent.
   - Sister Janie Hagen described him as close to his family and often helping
     their mother with shopping and errands.
   - Left his family's home for a friend's house on March 24, 1988.
   - Was carrying three dollars and no wallet according to the draft.
   - Family hired a private investigator who later defrauded them.
   - Decades later, Hagen publicly criticized Dahmer-themed tourism.
   - Keep the family-life material here; move tourism/media criticism to
     Impact / Media.

5. ANTHONY SEARS, 26
   - Had recently been made a manager at Baker Square.
   - Aspiring model.
   - Had a girlfriend he intended to marry after saving enough money.
   - Had told loved ones he would celebrate his promotion at Easter dinner.
   - Was killed in March 1989.
   - Verify age against source records before finalizing table.

6. RAYMOND "RICKY BEEKS" SMITH, 33
   - Generally known by the nickname Ricky Beeks.
   - Had recently been released from prison.
   - Was living with his half-sister in Rockford, Illinois.
   - Had a ten-year-old daughter.
   - Last seen in May 1990.
   - Need further biographical sourcing if fuller profile is desired.

7. EDWARD "EDDIE" SMITH, 28
   - Described as devoutly Christian and close with his mother Josephene and
     siblings JW and Carolyn.
   - Hoped to pursue modeling.
   - Nicknamed "the Sheikh" by friends because of the turban-like wraps he wore.
   - Sister Theresa later publicly confirmed that he was gay.
   - Reported missing in June 1990.
   - Verify age and exact wording around sexuality/family attribution.

8. ERNEST MILLER, 22
   - Dreamed of becoming a professional dancer.
   - Planned to enroll in an arts college in Chicago.
   - Was in Milwaukee visiting relatives when he was killed.
   - Killed in September 1990.
   - Verify age and details against primary/strong secondary sources.

9. DAVID THOMAS, 23
   - Had a two-year-old daughter, Courtia Beanland.
   - Ex-girlfriend Chandra Beanland later described the child's questions about
     when her father was coming home.
   - Friends remembered him as fun-loving.
   - Reported missing in late September 1990.
   - Verify exact chronology and attribution.

10. CURTIS STRAUGHTER, 18
    - Dropped out of high school.
    - Worked as a nursing assistant.
    - Lived with grandmother Catherine and mother Dorothy in Milwaukee.
    - Hoped to earn a GED and attend modeling school.
    - Grandmother said he often stayed out with friends, so his absence did not
      alarm her immediately.
    - Last seen in mid-February 1991.
    - Verify age discrepancy and exact last-seen date.

11. ERROL LINDSEY, 19
    - Youngest of six siblings.
    - Left behind a child born after his death.
    - Sister Rita Isbell later delivered a well-known victim-impact statement
      at trial.
    - Isbell later publicly criticized the 2022 Netflix dramatization.
    - The Netflix/media criticism belongs primarily in Impact / Media.
    - Disappeared in April 1991.

12. ANTHONY "TONY" HUGHES, 31
    - Deaf since childhood pneumonia.
    - Communicated through lip-reading and sign language.
    - Lived in Madison and was visiting family in Milwaukee.
    - Mother Shirley Hughes taught a Bible class at her church.
    - Told his mother he would stay overnight with a new friend named Jeffrey.
    - Mother later publicly criticized entertainment portrayals of the case.
    - Last seen leaving a Milwaukee gay bar on May 24, 1991.
    - Keep deafness and family/life material here; move media criticism to
      Impact / Media.

13. KONERAK SINTHASOMPHONE, 14
    - Born in 1976.
    - One of ten children.
    - Family fled Laos in 1979 following political change, crossing the Mekong
      into Thailand.
    - Spent roughly a year in a refugee camp before resettling in Wisconsin.
    - Family resettlement involved assistance from Milwaukee's Catholic
      Archdiocese.
    - Older siblings worked in factories/assembly lines as the family built a
      life in Wisconsin.
    - Older brother Somsack had been sexually assaulted by Dahmer in 1988.
    - Konerak's 1991 police encounter became central to later public scrutiny
      of institutional response.
    - His biography should be split conceptually:
        A. Who Konerak was / family history
        B. Why his case became historically significant
    - Detailed police-failure analysis belongs in Systemic Failures.

14. MATT TURNER, 20
    - Sometimes used the name Donald Montrell.
    - From Flint, Michigan.
    - Hoped to become a model.
    - Met Dahmer at a Chicago gay pride event.
    - Agreed to travel back with him.
    - Disappeared in late June 1991.
    - Verify exact date and source for alias/name usage.

15. JEREMIAH WEINBERGER, 23
    - Born in Puerto Rico.
    - Lived in Chicago.
    - Worked as a customer service representative for a video company.
    - Roommate described him as meticulous, neat, and careful about appearance.
    - Last seen July 6 after leaving a Chicago gay bar with Dahmer.
    - Dahmer said they traveled by Greyhound bus to Milwaukee and Weinberger
      stayed two days before he was killed.
    - Verify all dates and biographical attributions.

16. OLIVER LACY, 23
    - Youngest of three sons.
    - Had a two-year-old daughter.
    - Engaged to be married.
    - Raised by a single mother, Catherine.
    - Wore his late father's cross necklace as a keepsake.
    - Last seen in mid-July 1991.
    - Verify exact last-seen date and family details.

17. JOSEPH BRADEHOFT, 25
    - Had recently moved to Milwaukee.
    - Reportedly on his way to a job interview when he encountered Dahmer.
    - Father of three children, ages two to seven.
    - Final known victim in the series.
    - Killed in late July 1991 shortly before Tracy Edwards' escape.
    - Verify date and employment details.
