These may describe separate points in a late-night/after-midnight sequence, but the sources should not be flattened into a single unqualified date.

**Handling:** Keep March 25 as the last-seen date in the Victims plan. In a fuller timeline, separately label the reported last sighting, reported meeting, and subsequent journey if each is included. Do not silently change the frozen table entry to March 26.
