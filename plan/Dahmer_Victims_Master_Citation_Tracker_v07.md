### Rules we must not break

1. Do not edit or rewrite the frozen Victims content during citation work. Flag substantive corrections for separate approval.
2. Preserve the current Victims source numbers `[1]`–`[14]` in working notes. Assign consolidated final numbering only at the end.
3. Treat `[14]` (generic UPI Archives portal) as a **search portal**, not a claim-level citation. Cite a specific article for an exact claim.
4. Keep family/friend recollections attributed to their source/person.
5. Keep **last seen/last known alive**, **reported missing**, **alleged offense date**, **identification date**, and **next-of-kin notification** separate.
6. A criminal complaint records allegations and sworn statements; do not describe an allegation as a judicial finding.
7. Preserve contradictions. Do not manufacture precision or silently select one version.
8. An FBI page/image locator is not final until the exact linked PDF and its page system are checked.
9. No graphic detail or media controversy is needed for the biographies. For Tony Hughes, keep the profile life-centred and do not expand into the Netflix portrayal. Detailed Konerak institutional analysis belongs in **Systemic Failures**.
