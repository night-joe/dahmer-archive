LOCK DECLARATION & WHAT THIS DOCUMENT ADDS
================================================================================

The v0.2 working reference established the Overview's editorial structure,
voice, hierarchy, interaction model and hyperlink strategy. It deliberately
deferred sourcing. Its own CONTENT STATUS block recorded:

    "Final source-by-source article citations: NOT YET INSERTED"
    "Final web copy: NOT YET LOCKED"

This document closes that deferral. It does not rewrite the approved structure.
It adds the missing evidentiary layer:

1. A numbered Overview register ([1]-[41]) built entirely from sources already
   verified and locked in Tabs 2-7. No new, unvetted citations are introduced.
2. A section-by-section CLAIM INVENTORY assigning every factual assertion in
   the Overview a source number and, where the source permits, a locator.
3. A CROSSWALK mapping each Overview number to its parent tab register, so the
   eventual /sources page can resolve one global register without renumbering
   conflicts.
4. An EDITORIAL JUDGMENT FLAGS section identifying sentences in the approved
   v0.2 copy that cannot carry a citation because they are evaluative, and
   therefore must be revised or explicitly framed as the project's own
   assessment.

Everything in v0.2 concerning architecture, homepage behavior, expand/collapse
state, header routing, key-facts module and internal hyperlink targets is
carried forward unchanged.

================================================================================
SECTION A: EDITORIAL STANDARDS (CARRIED FORWARD, BINDING)
================================================================================

A.1 TONE
    Restrained scholarly/journalistic register. Prohibited in the site's own
    voice: "monster", "depraved predator", "domestic hell", "house of horrors",
    and equivalent sensational formulations.

A.2 STRUCTURAL SEPARATION
    HOMEPAGE OVERVIEW FEATURE = concise descriptive introduction.
    FULL /overview            = comprehensive case article and factual spine.
    They are two layers of one publication, not the same article at two lengths.

A.3 NAVIGATION
    Header "Overview" always opens /overview.
    Homepage feature expands in place and terminates with "Read more -> /overview".
    Expansion never navigates away from the homepage.

A.4 EVIDENTIARY CONDUCT
    - Distinguish 17 confessed killings from 16 murder convictions.
    - Distinguish a last-known sighting from an alleged offense date, a
      missing-person report date, and a forensic identification date.
    - Attribute disputed interpretations to their sources.
    - Never promote a later theory (journalistic, clinical, or cultural) into
      an established fact.

A.5 CERTAINTY TIERS (used throughout the Claim Inventory)
    [ESTABLISHED]  Primary record or court document directly establishes it.
    [DOCUMENTED]   Credible record supports it; contextual qualification needed.
    [REPORTED]     Published by a credible contemporary outlet; not independently
                   established.
    [ATTRIBUTED]   Asserted by a named person or party (e.g., Dahmer's own
                   confession, an expert's testimony, a witness statement).
    [DISPUTED]     Credible sources conflict; both versions must remain visible.
    [JUDGMENT]     The project's own analytical framing. Must never be presented
                   as a sourced fact.

SECTION D: EDITORIAL JUDGMENT FLAGS
================================================================================

The following sentences appear in the approved v0.2 homepage copy but CANNOT
carry a citation, because they are evaluative rather than factual. Each must be
either revised to a sourced formulation or explicitly framed as the project's
assessment. They are listed here so the Phase 2 copywriter does not silently
promote them.

FLAG 1
  v0.2 TEXT: "the case had become one of the most extensive and consequential
             serial-murder investigations in the United States."
  PROBLEM:   Superlative ranking. No source in the register establishes it.
  ACTION:    Replace with a sourced scope statement, e.g. the case spanned
             multiple jurisdictions, 16 convictions and a documented federal
             civil-rights aftermath [1], [2], [3], [7]. If a ranking is
             retained, it must be attributed to a specific source.

FLAG 2
  v0.2 TEXT: "Many of Dahmer's victims were young men from marginalized
             communities, including a large number of Black victims..."
  PROBLEM:   "Large number" is vague and unfalsifiable in that form.
  ACTION:    Replace with Claim H.5's quantified form: 11 of 17 victims were
             Black [8], [12].

FLAG 3
  v0.2 TEXT: "Many encounters occurred around Milwaukee's LGBTQ nightlife..."
  PROBLEM:   Overgeneralizes. The record documents some venues and some victims.
  ACTION:    Use the narrowed Claim H.7 formulation.

FLAG 4
  v0.2 TEXT: "making questions of visibility, vulnerability and institutional
             response an important part of its later examination."
  PROBLEM:   Legitimate analysis, but presented as though it were a finding.
  ACTION:    Mark as the project's framing (Claim H.8, [JUDGMENT]).

FLAG 5
  CONCLUSION: "The significance of the case extends beyond the crimes
             themselves."
  PROBLEM:   Editorial thesis.
  ACTION:    Permitted in a conclusion, but must read as interpretation. Do not
             attach a citation that implies a source asserts it.

SECTION G: CONSISTENCY CONSTRAINTS FOR PHASE 2
================================================================================

G.1 THE THREE NUMBERS
    The Overview will reference three different figures in close proximity:
        17 = killings Dahmer confessed to
        16 = murder convictions (15 Wisconsin + 1 Ohio)
        11 = victims whose remains were recovered from Apartment 213
    These must never be used interchangeably. Every use must carry its own
    defining noun. See Claims 1.2, 1.3, 3.5.

G.2 MILESTONE SEPARATION
    For any victim mentioned in the Overview:
        last-known sighting != missing-person report != alleged offense date
        != forensic identification date.
    The Overview should avoid naming specific dates for individual victims
    altogether, deferring to Table 1. This removes an entire class of error.

G.3 KILLING PERIOD BOUNDARIES
    The project-wide formula is "1978-1991". The first killing is 1978 (Hicks);
    the arrest is July 22, 1991; the last killing is July 1991 (Bradehoft).
    Do not extend the span to 1992 because the trial ran that year.

G.4 LEGAL FRAMING
    Never "convicted by a jury" for the Wisconsin counts. He pleaded; the jury
    adjudicated responsibility. See B.7.

G.5 SETTLEMENT FRAMING
    Any mention of the Sinthasomphone civil settlement carries the phrase
    "without admission of liability". See Claim 9.3.

G.6 NO UNDUE WEIGHT ON ONE SOURCE
    The Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective [12] is the richest secondary
    source in the register. No single sentence of the Overview should rest on it
    alone where a primary record exists.
