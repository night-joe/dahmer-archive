Do not change the Victims table’s last-seen date to reflect an alleged offense date. If the full Timeline page includes both entries, label the date types and identify the source disagreement.

### Age discrepancy — internal FBI inconsistency

The prior verification report flagged Oliver Lacy’s birth-date discrepancy, but the correct handling is more specific than “FBI DOB implies age 24”:

- **MJS / contemporary press:** commonly report age 23.
- **FBI Vault Part 01:** identifies Lacy as age 23 and gives DOB June 23, 1968.
- **FBI Vault Part 06:** the verification report notes a June 23, 1967 entry, which would imply age 24 at the relevant time.

This is an apparent inconsistency inside the FBI file, not simply disagreement between the FBI and MJS. Before the final website note is locked, confirm the exact Part 06 page/image and quote the entry accurately. Until then, keep the asterisk and describe the records as inconsistent; do not silently pick a birth year.

### Last-seen vs. alleged encounter/offense date

- **July 12, 1991:** MJS and UPI’s August 5, 1991 report say Lacy was last seen alive on this date.
- **July 15, 1991:** the July 25 UPI report summarizing the criminal complaint says Dahmer met Lacy on July 15.
- **July 24, 1991:** UPI reported that police released Lacy’s identity and noted fingerprint identification.

These are different events: last seen, alleged encounter/offense, and identification after arrest. The profile should keep **July 12** labelled as last known/last seen, not substitute July 15.

### Recommended citation action

- Retain [1] for biography and the principal age shown in the profile.
- Use direct UPI coverage instead of generic source-directory [14]:
  - July 24, 1991: https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991-07-24/Suspect-in-multiple-slayings-says-he-responsible/5763680328000/
  - July 25, 1991 complaint report: https://www.upi.com/amp/Archives/1991/07/25/Complaint-Man-killed-victims-saved-heart-to-eat/3475680414400/
  - August 5, 1991 last-seen/lawsuit report: https://www.upi.com/Archives/1991/08/05/Family-of-Dahmer-victim-files-lawsuit/6324681364800/
- Anchor both FBI birth-date entries to exact part and page/image before publishing the discrepancy note: https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer
- Do not use Wikipedia, memorial-timeline sites, or unsourced victim lists as evidence when the MJS retrospective, complaint, FBI file, or contemporary reporting is available.

**Verdict:** Biographical claims are mapped to the principal source. Keep age discrepancy visible and verify the Part 06 FBI image. Preserve July 12 as last-seen date and separate it from July 15 complaint chronology.
