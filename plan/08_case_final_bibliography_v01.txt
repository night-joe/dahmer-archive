SCOPE NOTE & DUAL-SPECIFICATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. DUAL-SPECIFICATION MODULAR STRUCTURE:
   Tab 5 (Case) of the Jeffrey Dahmer Case Research Webapp is structurally partitioned
   into two locked specification documents:
   - Part A: The Offense Series (`plan/03a_case_offense_series_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`)
     Covers the 17 victim homicides, 5 prior offenses/contacts, strict 4-milestone separation,
     1-based FBI Vault physical page coordinates, and statutory homicide classifications.
   - Part B: Investigation, Arrest & Trial Proceedings (`plan/03b_case_investigation_and_trial_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`)
     Covers the July 22, 1991 Tracy Edwards escape, MPD Apartment 213 entry and search,
     60-hour detective interrogations, administrative actions against officers, charging
     evolution (4 -> 12 -> 15 counts), Ohio indictment, bifurcated trial under Wis. Stat.
     § 971.15 & § 971.165, expert psychiatric testimonies, 10–2 jury sanity verdict,
     sentencing proceedings, and post-trial litigation.

2. BIBLIOGRAPHY REGISTERS & CITATION CITING CONVENTIONS:
   To maintain 100% evidentiary integrity without destabilizing cross-tab citations:
   - Part A Register ([1]–[32]): Formatted according to the project-wide master register
     harmonized across Table 1 (Victims Tab 04) and Table 2 (Timeline Tab 05), where [1] is
     the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective, [2] is the FBI Vault primary record,
     [3] is the Milwaukee County criminal complaint, etc.
   - Part B Register ([1]–[33]): Formatted as an authoritative standalone trial and
     investigation register, beginning with primary investigative and court dockets ([1]–[3]),
     statutory authorities ([4]–[8]), Court TV trial archives ([9]), and contemporaneous
     press coverage of trial proceedings ([10]–[33]).
   - Master Unified Register: This document (`08_case_final_bibliography_v01.txt`) constitutes
     the definitive harmonization bridge between both registers, providing an exhaustive
     bidirectional crosswalk, deduplicated master listings, and evidentiary verification notes.

SECTION 5: SOURCE VERIFICATION NOTES — OPEN ITEMS PRESERVED
================================================================================

1. STEVEN TUOMI CORPUS DELICTI & UNCHARGED STATUS:
   - Extrajudicial Confession: During post-arrest debriefings with Detectives Kennedy and
     Murphy, Dahmer confessed to killing Steven Tuomi at the Ambassador Hotel (FBI Part 03, p. 10).
     Dahmer stated the event occurred "somewhere around 1986 or 1987," whereas missing-person
     reports established Tuomi was last seen alive on September 15, 1987 (Marc's Big Boy).
   - Legal Corpus Delicti Threshold: Under Wisconsin Supreme Court precedent (*State v. Verhasselt*,
     83 Wis. 2d 647, 266 N.W.2d 342 (1978)), an extrajudicial confession cannot support a bindover
     or conviction without independent corroborating evidence of corpus delicti. Because Dahmer
     disposed of Tuomi's remains in the trash and zero biological or physical trace evidence was
     recovered (FBI Part 01, pp. 43, 60; Part 06, pp. 3, 15), District Attorney E. Michael McCann
     formally declined to file homicide charges on August 22, 1991.
   - Editorial Mandate: Webapp text preserves Tuomi as one of the 17 homicide victims while
     strictly differentiating him from the 15 charged Milwaukee homicides.

2. STATUTORY SPLIT UNDER 1987 WISCONSIN ACT 399:
   - Effective Date: January 1, 1989. Act 399 overhauled Wisconsin criminal homicide law,
     repealing former Wis. Stat. § 940.01 ("First-Degree Murder") and enacting Wis. Stat.
     § 940.01(1) ("First-Degree Intentional Homicide").
   - Charging Allocation:
     * Pre-1989 Offenses (2 Counts First-Degree Murder): Count 13 (James Doxtator, Jan 1988)
       and Count 14 (Richard Guerrero, March 1988).
     * Post-1989 Offenses (13 Counts First-Degree Intentional Homicide): Counts 01–12 and 15
       (Anthony Sears through Joseph Bradehoft).
     * Non-Wisconsin Offense: Steven Hicks charged under Ohio Rev. Code § 2903.01 ("Aggravated Murder").

3. FOUR-MILESTONE SEPARATION ACROSS ALL 17 VICTIMS:
   Every victim profile in Tab 5 strictly maintains temporal and institutional boundaries between:
   (1) Last Known Sighting Date (witness, friend, or family contact)
   (2) Missing-Person Report Date (police department intake)
   (3) Alleged Offense Date (prosecutorial complaint allegation)
   (4) Forensic Identification Date (dental, fingerprint, or anthropological match).
   Example: Curtis Straughter grandmother last saw him Feb 18, 1991; reported missing

5. WISCONSIN SANITY TRIAL MECHANICS (WIS. STAT. § 971.15 & § 805.09(2)):
   - Trial Bifurcation: On January 13, 1992, Dahmer entered a plea of guilty but not guilty
     by reason of mental disease or defect (NGI) across all 15 Milwaukee counts. Under
     Wis. Stat. § 971.165, this waived the guilt phase, leaving only the sanity phase to be tried.
   - Burden of Proof: Under Wis. Stat. § 971.15(3), legal insanity is an affirmative defense
     where the defendant bears the civil burden of proving lack of substantial capacity by the
     greater weight of the credible evidence (preponderance).
   - Five-Sixths Jury Rule: Under Wis. Stat. § 805.09(2), a sanity verdict in Wisconsin requires
     only 10 of 12 jurors to agree. On February 15, 1992, the jury returned a 10–2 verdict finding
     Dahmer legally sane across all 15 counts.
