- **Demolition & Site Clearance:** The 49-unit building was purchased on August 13, 1992 by the Campus Circle Project (a private nonprofit redevelopment corporation affiliated with Marquette University) for $325,000 and demolished between November 14 and 16, 1992 to prevent commercial exploitation and macabre tourism (Timeline Anchor `#event-1992-oxford-demolition`).

### 3. Verification of Core Editorial Standard: Non-Deterministic Framing

Both `02_context_content_plan_v02.txt` and `00_webapp_master_implementation_plan_v04.txt` mandate a strict editorial policy:
- **Core Principle:** Macroeconomic restructuring, racial segregation, neighborhood poverty, sexual minority stigma, parental divorce, childhood hernia surgery, and adolescent alcohol abuse provide the *setting* and *institutional environment* surrounding the murders. They must **never** be presented as direct, deterministic causes of Dahmer's crimes.
- **Evidentiary Boundary:** The audit confirms that maintaining strict non-deterministic framing requires four distinct analytical distinctions in every subsection:
  1. *Documented Historical Conditions:* Empirically verified facts (e.g., Census segregation indices, plant closures, military counseling records).
  2. *Contemporary Observations:* Real-time 1991 reporting and statements reflecting early impressions and municipal reactions.
  3. *Attributed Recollections:* Family and acquaintance recollections (Lionel Dahmer memoir, schoolmates) explicitly qualified as subjective personal memories.
  4. *Retrospective Interpretations:* Post-1991 criminological, psychiatric, and sociological analyses (e.g., Steven Egger's "less dead" theory, diagnostic evaluations) presented strictly as scholarly analytical models, never as objective facts or defendant motives.

## Unresolved Discrepancies

The comparative audit identified four substantive discrepancies across primary records, demographic studies, and secondary accounts that cannot be resolved through single-source selection and must be transparently preserved:

### 1. Conflicting Primary Dates for James Doxtator's Disappearance
- **Discrepancy:** FBI Vault Part 01, p. 43 records detectives' initial notes placing an alleged homicide on **January 18, 1988**, and the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective reports his disappearance as **January 16, 1988**. However, FBI Vault Part 01, p. 60 (teletype DE FBIMW 0011, August 23, 1991) explicitly states that Doxtator disappeared in the **"spring of 1988."**
- **Impact on Age Calculation:** Doxtator was born on March 1, 1973 (FBI Part 01, p. 43). If he disappeared on January 16/18, 1988, he was **14 years, 10 months old**. If he disappeared in the "spring of 1988" (after March 1), he was **15 years old**.
- **Audit Treatment:** Preserved as an unresolved primary source discrepancy. Both `04_victims` (v05 locked) and `05_timeline` (v04 locked) mark his age as `14*` to denote this documented conflict. Section B of `02_context_content_plan_v03_final_locked.txt` must note both dates.

### 2. Steven Tuomi Homicide Chronology and Missing Corpus Delicti
- **Discrepancy:** Steven Tuomi was last seen alive leaving his job at Marc's Big Boy restaurant on **September 15, 1987** (FBI Part 01, pp. 43, 53). However, during interrogations, Dahmer was unable to provide a precise date for the homicide at the Ambassador Hotel, stating it occurred "somewhere around 1986 or 1987" (FBI Part 03, p. 10). Furthermore, an MPD evidence transmittal in Part 06 (p. 3) references crimes committed "between November 1987 and July 19, 1991."
- **Legal & Evidentiary Consequence:** Because no physical remains were ever located, Milwaukee County District Attorney E. Michael McCann concluded that corpus delicti could not be established under Wisconsin law; consequently, Dahmer was never charged with Tuomi's death in Wisconsin.
- **Audit Treatment:** Preserved as an unresolved temporal window (`November 1987*` / `1987–1988`). Tab 4 must explain this evidentiary limitation without declaring an unverified exact date of death.

### 3. Divergent 1990 U.S. Census Tract Demographics for Avenues West
- **Discrepancy:** Various historical studies and planning reports analyzing the Near West Side surrounding 924 N. 25th Street cite differing demographic percentages for rental occupancy, poverty rates, and racial composition in 1990. These divergences stem from whether the researcher analyzed Census Tract 139 specifically, aggregated Tracts 138, 139, and 140, or evaluated the broader Near West Side planning area.
- **Audit Treatment:** In `02_context_content_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`, report demographic and housing figures with explicit tract qualification (Census Tract 139 / Avenues West residential tract) rather than asserting blanket, unanchored percentages.

### 4. Attributed Family Memoir Claims vs. Objective Documentary Evidence
- **Discrepancy:** In *A Father's Story* (1994), Lionel Dahmer attributes a profound personality change in Jeffrey Dahmer to a double-hernia operation performed shortly before his fourth birthday in early 1964. Secondary popular accounts have frequently repeated this recollection as an established medical cause of Dahmer's emotional detachment.
- **Evidentiary Standard:** Medical and psychiatric records in FBI Vault Parts 01 and 03 confirm the surgical procedure but provide zero evidentiary foundation for a causal neurological or psychological transformation.
- **Audit Treatment:** In accordance with the project's non-deterministic editorial mandate, Lionel Dahmer's account must be explicitly cited as an *attributed parental recollection*, with a clear disclaimer that medical records do not support any causal transformation.
