### Age and date qualifications

- **Age:** Victims [1] reports 24. FBI record / criminal complaint lists a date of birth of **May 5, 1967**, which would make Miller **23** on September 2 or 3, 1990. Preserve the `24*` age note rather than choosing silently.
- **Last-seen versus alleged offense:** The content plan says last seen September 2. UPI’s July 30 report says he disappeared September 2, while the criminal complaint records aunt Vivian Miller’s statement that he came from Chicago on September 1 and left her home during the **early morning hours of September 3**, after which she did not see or hear from him. The complaint separately alleges the killing occurred on September 3.
- **Recommended audit handling:** Do not automatically overwrite the plan’s September 2 last-seen entry. Record both sources and the distinction. The discrepancy may reflect a date-boundary or reporting difference, but that is not established by the current evidence. Final wording should identify the source basis instead of presenting the explanation as fact.

### Citation actions

1. Keep **[1]** for his education, dance aspirations, and aunt’s recollections.
2. Keep **[2]** for the FBI DOB note, with an exact Part/page or image anchor to be confirmed at final citation stage.
3. Add a specific contemporary citation to UPI **“Dahmer reportedly made terror phone calls” (July 30, 1991)** for the reported disappearance date and his grandmother’s phone-call account; use the complaint to distinguish the aunt’s statement about his departure in the early hours of September 3.
4. Add UPI **“Suit seeks $2 billion from alleged mass killer” (August 9, 1991)** only where a contemporary account of the complaint and Labor Day visit is needed; avoid its civil-lawsuit framing for routine biographical claims.
5. Maintain a note that the listed last-seen date and the alleged killing date are distinct concepts.

### Case chronology and remains

Contemporary UPI reporting on July 30, 1991 lists Thomas among Dahmer’s identified victims even though his body had not been found and says he had been missing since September 1990. UPI’s August 6 report says the complaint alleged that he was killed in autumn 1990 and records that his girlfriend, who reported him missing that September, identified him through photographs found in Dahmer’s apartment. These support the broader case context, but the alleged offense period (“autumn 1990”) is not the same as the exact last-seen date (September 24).

### Citation actions

1. Keep **[1]** for the family, daughter, and exact September 24 last-seen date.
2. Add specific contemporary UPI articles **“Dahmer reportedly made terror phone calls” (July 30, 1991)** and **“Dahmer charged with dozen killings” (August 6, 1991)** as corroboration for reported-missing status, the absence of recovered remains, and the identification through photographs.
3. Use **[9]** only if the profile explicitly refers to a family victim-impact statement; the current short biography does not need to import the fuller statement.
4. Retain the instruction to remove the draft phrase “passionate hustler.” It is vague, adds little biographical value, and is not needed to describe his family or character.
5. Do not use the alleged autumn 1990 killing period as a substitute for the September 24 last-seen date.
