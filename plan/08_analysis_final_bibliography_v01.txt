1. TRIPARTITE MODULAR SPECIFICATION STRUCTURE:
   Tab 6 (Analysis) of the Jeffrey Dahmer Case Research Webapp is partitioned into
   three locked content specifications:
   - Part A: Psychological Profile & Forensic Evaluation (`plan/06a_analysis_psychological_profile_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`)
     Covers the clinical evaluation methodology (>200 hours of interviews, IQ 115-120, MMPI),
     the foundational divide between clinical illness and legal sanity under Wis. Stat. § 971.15,
     the 7 key testifying expert witnesses, the diagnostic matrix, control/trepanning experiments,
     the 10-2 jury sanity verdict, epistemological boundaries, and homosexuality decoupling.
   - Part B: Systemic Failures & Institutional Contacts (`plan/06b_analysis_systemic_failures_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`)
     Covers the May 27, 1991 Konerak Sinthasomphone police encounter, paramedic dismissal, 911/radio
     audio recordings, Glenda Cleveland's follow-up calls, officer disciplinary proceedings and state
     court reinstatement, federal civil rights litigation (Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee),
     probation supervision under agent Donna Chester (Weinberger v. Wisconsin) and written home-visit
     waivers under Wis. Admin. Code § DOC 328.04, Oxford Apartments odor complaints, Steven Egger's
     "less-dead" victimology framework, and the 6-dimensional systemic failure framework.
   - Part C: Theories & Motivations: Competing Explanations (`plan/06c_analysis_theories_and_motivations_plan_v03_final_locked.txt`)
     Covers the 3 tiers of epistemological certainty, the 7 competing motivational theories (Possession &
     Control, Behavioral Addiction / Lankford & Hayes 2022 SUD analogy and 1993 Inside Edition self-report,
     Alcohol as Instrumental Tool / Dietz, Sexual Gratification / Necrophilia, Rejection & Abandonment /
     Palermo, Destructive Hostility / Jentzen et al. 1994 withdrawn paper, and Longitudinal Behavioral
     Escalation across 3 phases), cross-theory synthesis matrix, and record boundaries.

--- TIER 1: PRIMARY LEGAL DOCKETS & JUDICIAL OPINIONS ---

[SRC-01] Estate of Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee, 838 F. Supp. 1320 (E.D. Wis. Nov. 23, 1993)
         (Judge Terence T. Evans). Primary federal qualified immunity decision documenting the
         factual sequence of the May 27, 1991 Konerak Sinthasomphone police encounter, dismissal of
         paramedics, Dahmer's deception, and subsequent homicide 30 minutes later.
         URL: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/838/1320/2255276/

[SRC-02] Estate of Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee, 785 F. Supp. 1343 (E.D. Wis. March 5, 1992)
         (Judge Terence T. Evans). Primary federal decision on motion to dismiss, holding that
         complaint stated valid Equal Protection claims regarding differential police protection
         based on race, national origin, and sexual orientation; notes 11 of 17 victims were Black.
         URL: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/785/1343/2144838/

[SRC-03] Weinberger v. Wisconsin, 105 F.3d 1182 (7th Cir. Jan. 23, 1997) (affirming 906 F. Supp. 485)
         (Judge Richard D. Cudahy). Authoritative circuit court decision evaluating probation agent
         Donna Chester's supervision, the written home-visit waiver system under Wis. Admin. Code §
         DOC 328.04, and holding Chester did not act with deliberate indifference.
         URL: https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/105/105.F3d.1182.95-3398.html

[SRC-04] Balcerzak v. City of Milwaukee, 163 F.3d 993 (7th Cir. Dec. 18, 1998) (affirming 980 F. Supp. 983)
         (Judge Terence T. Evans). Comprehensive procedural history of officer disciplinary proceedings:
         July 1991 suspension, September 1991 discharge, October 1992 FPC pleas, April 1994 state
         circuit court reinstatement by Reserve Judge Robert Parins, and dismissal of officers' § 1983 claims.
         URL: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/163/993/494395/

[SRC-05] Estate of Sinthasomphone v. City of Milwaukee, 878 F. Supp. 147 (E.D. Wis. 1995)
         (Judge Terence T. Evans). Pre-trial evidentiary ruling on equal protection claims and
         municipal liability prior to final municipal settlement.
         URL: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/878/147/1440090/

[SRC-06] State of Wisconsin v. Jeffrey L. Dahmer, Case No. F-912542 (Milwaukee County Circuit Court).
         Official court record: Initial Complaint (July 25, 1991), Information (Aug 22, 1991),
         Guilty Plea (Jan 13, 1992), Sanity Phase Trial Transcripts (Jan 30 – Feb 15, 1992 before
         Judge Laurence C. Gram Jr.), and Sentencing (Feb 17, 1992).

--- TIER 5: PEER-REVIEWED SCHOLARLY & HISTORICAL MONOGRAPHS ---

[SRC-32] Lankford, Adam, & Hayes, Jenna K. (2022).
         "Could Serial Killing Actually Be Addictive? A Close Examination of Compulsion and Escalation
         in the Jeffrey Dahmer Case." Sexual Health & Compulsivity, 29(3–4): 198–224.
         DOI: 10.1080/26929953.2022.2126416. Peer-reviewed empirical analysis applying DSM-5 SUD
         criteria by analogy to model compulsion, tolerance, and longitudinal behavioral escalation.

[SRC-33] O'Meara, Gregory J., S.J. (2009).
         "Text, Context, and Pretext in State v. Jeffrey Dahmer." 42 Marquette Law Review.
         Scholarly legal analysis of courtroom narrative construction, cross-examination strategies,
         and expert witness dynamics during the 1992 sanity trial.
         URL: https://scholarship.law.marquette.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1128&context=facpub

[SRC-34] Jentzen, Jeffrey (2017).
         "Micro Disasters: The Case of Serial Killer Jeffrey Dahmer." Academic Forensic Pathology,
         7(3): 355–368. Comprehensive forensic pathology synthesis covering victim identification,
         trepanning burr-hole pathology, chemical dissolution, and scene recovery logistics.
         URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6474573/

[SRC-35] Steven Egger (2002).
         "The Less-Dead." SAGE Encyclopedia of Violent Crime. Analytical framework examining how
         marginalized, stigmatized, or disenfranchised victim populations experience reduced social
         visibility, delayed police investigations, and heightened vulnerability to serial predators.
         URL: https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/violentcrime/chpt/lessdead
