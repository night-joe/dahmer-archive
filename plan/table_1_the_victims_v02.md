## Editorial & Methodological Standard

1. **Human-Centered Approach:** This table focuses on the lives, identities, relationships, and aspirations of the victims. Forensic and sensationalized murder details are excluded.
2. **Chronological Structure:** Entries are arranged chronologically by the best-supported reported last-known date or period, not by any perceived hierarchy.
3. **No Implied Date of Death:** The table does not use “Date of death.” A last-known sighting, missing-person report, alleged offense date, and forensic identification date are different events and must not be conflated.
4. **Age Convention:** The preferred age is age on the best-supported last-known date, calculated from a documented birth date where available. If birth-date records conflict, use an asterisk (`*`) and explain the discrepancy. If a sufficiently supported birth date is not available in this register, retain the historically reported age and mark it with a dagger (`†`) to show it has not been independently recalculated here.
5. **Date Qualification:** Where the exact day is not independently corroborated or the record identifies only a disappearance/reporting period, use the broader period and explain the limitation. Do not present a reported disappearance period as a confirmed last sighting.
6. **Claim-Level Sourcing:** Citations use the authoritative Victims bibliography numbering `[1]`–`[18]`. Additional sources unique to this table are assigned new numbers `[19]`–`[27]`; existing bibliography numbers are not reused for different publications.
7. **Audit Transparency:** Conflicting ages, dates, and source descriptions remain visible in the audit notes rather than being silently reconciled.

## Methodological Framework & Audit Notes

### 1. Distinction of Record Dates
The dates presented above reflect the best-supported reported last-known sighting or contact where one is established. Some entries instead show a broader disappearance period or a significant police encounter; these are labelled explicitly. In case research, the following distinct milestones must not be conflated:
1. **Last Known Date:** The final reported date family members, friends, or witnesses saw or spoke with the victim alive; it may remain disputed or unconfirmed.
2. **Missing Person Report Date:** The date local law enforcement formally accepted a missing-person report (often days or weeks after the disappearance).
3. **Alleged Offense Date:** The approximate date of homicide specified in the Milwaukee County District Attorney's charging complaints, largely derived from Dahmer's post-arrest interrogations.
4. **Forensic Identification Date:** The date authorities established identity using methods such as fingerprint comparison, dental records, or forensic anthropology.

### 2. Itemized Victim Audit Notes

* **Steven Hicks:**
  * *Last Known Date:* The June 18, 1978 date is derived from the *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1]. FBI Vault records (Part 01, pp. 24–25) record Hicks as a possible Ohio victim and document a June 24, 1978 Ohio missing-person report; the June 24 missing report is a distinct administrative event and must not be used to substantiate the June 18 sighting.
  * *Birth Date Discrepancy:* FBI Part 01 lists DOB as June 27, 1959, whereas other internal summaries list June 22, 1959. Both dates establish his age as 18 on June 18, 1978.
  * *Forensic Recovery:* Hicks's remains were recovered in Bath Township, Ohio, in August 1991 and identified in September 1991 [4, 17].

* **Steven Tuomi:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`24*`):* The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1] and 1991 press accounts report age 28, consistent with age at later reporting. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 43 records detectives' belief that Tuomi's DOB was December 19, 1962, which implies age 24 on September 15, 1987. The table uses 24* under the age-at-last-known-date convention; the source-reported age of 28 remains documented here.
  * *Death Chronology & Legal Status:* Dahmer's confession stated the homicide occurred at the Ambassador Hotel "somewhere around 1986 or 1987" [2 (Part 03, PDF p. 10)]. Because no physical remains were recovered, prosecutors lacked independent corpus delicti; Tuomi was the only Milwaukee victim for whom no formal criminal charge was filed [26, 27].

* **James "Jamie" Doxtator:**
  * *Last Known Date:* The public-facing table uses January 1988 [1, 14]. The *Milwaukee Journal Sentinel* retrospective [1] gives January 16, but the identified contemporary UPI source [14] supports only the month. Retain the exact day in research notes unless independent day-level corroboration is found.
  * *Investigative Descriptors:* FBI Part 01, PDF p. 43 documents detectives' notes on "Jamie E. Doxator" with DOB March 1, 1973 (confirming age 14). The FBI notes describe him as "a Hispanic male," whereas family records and news retrospectives confirm Stockbridge and Oneida ancestry [1].

* **Richard Guerrero:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`22*`):* Three distinct ages appear across sources: MJS reports age 25 [1]; UPI (July 30, 1991 [11]) reports age 23 at disappearance; FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 13 records DOB December 12, 1965, implying age 22 during March 1988. The table uses 22* under the age-at-last-known-period convention, while preserving the reported-age discrepancy.
  * *Disappearance Period:* Guerrero was last seen in late March 1988; the formal missing-person report was accepted March 29, 1988 [2].

* **Anthony Sears:**
  * *Age Verification:* Corrected from earlier working drafts that listed age 26. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 24 records DOB January 28, 1965, confirming age 24 on March 25, 1989.
  * *Timeline Dissonance:* MJS retrospective records last seen March 25, 1989 [1]. UPI reports he met Dahmer on March 26 and planned to visit family for Easter on March 27. The criminal complaint alleges homicide on or about March 26 [3]. Easter Sunday was March 26, 1989, reflecting minor date confusion in contemporaneous press accounts.

* **Raymond "Ricky Beeks" Smith:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`32*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 33 [1]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 25 records DOB August 10, 1957, which implies age 32 on the May 29, 1990 last-seen date. The table uses 32* and preserves the reported age of 33 as a reference-date difference.
  * *Naming Convention:* The entry combines his legal name and commonly used moniker ("Ricky Beeks") to preserve family recognition and public record consistency.

* **Edward "Eddie" Smith:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`24*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 28 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 13 records a DOB of August 2, 1962, implying age 27 during June 1990. The table uses 27* and preserves the reported age of 28 as a reference-date difference.
  * *Last Known Date:* Reported missing in June 1990 [1, 11]. Shortly after, his sister Carolyn received an anonymous phone call stating Eddie was dead [1, 11].

* **Ernest Miller:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`23*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 24 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 12 records DOB May 5, 1967, implying age 23 on September 2, 1990. The table uses 23* and preserves the reported age of 24 as a reference-date difference.
  * *Chronology Separation:* MJS and UPI [1, 11] report last seen September 2, 1990. The Milwaukee County complaint [3] records aunt Vivian Miller's statement that Ernest left home around 1:30 a.m. on September 3, was reported missing September 4, and alleges homicide on or about September 3.

* **David Thomas:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 23. Disappeared September 24, 1990; missing person report filed by partner Chandra Beanland in September 1990 [1, 11].

* **Curtis Straughter:**
  * *Age Reference Difference (`17*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 18 [1]. FBI Vault Part 01, PDF p. 25 records DOB April 16, 1973, making him 17 on both the February 18 sighting and the March 7 date reported by MJS. UPI [10] reports him as 18 at post-arrest identification.
  * *Last Sighting Conflict:* MJS gives last seen March 7, 1991 [1]. FBI Part 06, PDF p. 11 and the criminal complaint [3] record his grandmother's last sighting as February 18, 1991. The formal missing report was filed July 24, 1991 [2].

* **Errol Lindsey:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 19. Last seen April 7, 1991 [1]. Family impact statement at sentencing delivered by sister Rita Isbell [8].

* **Anthony "Tony" Hughes:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 31. Last seen May 24, 1991 [1]. Sourced life profile emphasizes his active communication skills, bilingual signing/lip-reading, and close family ties [1, 8].

* **Konerak Sinthasomphone:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 14. Reported missing May 1991; police encounter occurred May 27, 1991 [1, 20, 22].
  * *Contextual Rule:* Family arrival is documented as 1980 [1, 19]. Deep analysis of the May 27 police failure is maintained in the Systemic Failures section to avoid reducing his biographical profile purely to institutional negligence.

* **Matt Turner:**
  * *Identity & Age Verification:* Age 20. Last seen June 30, 1991 [1, 23]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 confirms alias Donald Montrell and DOB July 3, 1970.

* **Jeremiah Weinberger:**
  * *Record Verification:* Age 23. Last seen July 6, 1991 [1, 23]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 confirms DOB September 29, 1967.

* **Oliver Lacy:**
  * *Age Discrepancy (`23–24*`):* MJS retrospective reports age 23 [1]. FBI Vault Part 06, PDF p. 9 records DOB June 23, 1967, implying age 24 on July 12, 1991. An earlier FBI entry in Part 01 implies 1968, which implies age 23. The table displays 23–24* rather than silently selecting one birth year.
  * *Chronology Separation:* Last seen July 12, 1991 [1, 23]; reported missing July 15, 1991; identified via fingerprints July 24, 1991 [2].

* **Joseph Bradehoft:**
  * *Record Consistency:* Age 25. Last seen July 16, 1991 [1, 23]. Final known victim; encountered near Marquette University campus [1, 21].
