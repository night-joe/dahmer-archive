### C. Anthony Sears: March 25/26 Chronology & Easter Sunday Calendar Conflict
* **Table Claim:** Last known date: `March 25, 1989 [1]`. Audit note records: Sears met Dahmer post-closing early March 26; Count 01 alleges homicide on/about March 26 [3]; UPI reports meeting night of March 26 and returning home for Easter on March 27 [14].
* **Audit of Cited Sources:**
  * **MJS Retrospective (2022, [1]):** Records Sears was last seen March 25, 1989.
  * **Milwaukee County Amended Criminal Complaint (Count 01, pp. 1, 3, [3]):** Witness Jeffrey Connor swore that he and Sears were at a tavern on the evening of March 25, 1989; after the bar closed (in the early hours of March 26), Sears met "Jeff" outside and left with him. Count 01 formally charges murder occurring *"on or about March 26, 1989"*.
  * **FBI Part 06, PDF Page 12 [2]:** MPD summary records: *"He was last seen at home on March 25, 1989, at 9:30 A.M., and reported missing to the Milwaukee Police Department by his friend on March 29, 1989."*
  * **UPI (August 16, 1991, [14]):** Reports police placed the meeting with Dahmer on the night of March 26 and that Sears told his mother he would be home for Easter on March 27.
* **Support Level:** **Direct** across sources for their respective claims, but reveals a calendar error in UPI:
  * Western Easter Sunday in 1989 fell on **March 26, 1989** (U.S. Naval Observatory / Astronomical Calendar).
  * UPI's statement that Sears planned to visit for "Easter on March 27" reflects journalistic confusion with Easter Monday.
* **Conclusion:** Table 1 correctly displays `March 25, 1989` (the last corroborated time family/witnesses saw him prior to his encounter with Dahmer) and documents the evening-to-early-morning transition in the audit notes.

# PART II: CHRONOLOGY & EVENT-TYPE CLASSIFICATION AUDIT (TASK 2)

A rigorous historical register must not collapse distinct legal and personal milestones into an implied "date of death." This section classifies every dated milestone across the 17 cases into a standardized six-category event taxonomy.

## 1. Six-Category Event Taxonomy
* **Category A: Last-Known Sighting / Eyewitness Contact** (physical sighting by family, friend, or acquaintance).
* **Category B: Disappearance Period** (broader unconfirmed window when specific daily contact ceased).
* **Category C: Missing-Person Report** (administrative date a formal report was logged with police).
* **Category D: Alleged Offense Date** (charging date in Milwaukee County Criminal Complaint or confession).
* **Category E: Police Encounter** (significant administrative/street interaction prior to arrest).
* **Category F: Forensic Identification Date & Notification** (dental/fingerprint match and family notice).
