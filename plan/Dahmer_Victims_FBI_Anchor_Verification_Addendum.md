## 1. Critical locator issue: “PDF page” must mean the actual linked PDF's page index

The evidence report labels several locators as PDF page indexes—for example, FBI Part 01 PDF pages 170 and 202. However, opening the FBI Vault's standalone Part 01 PDF reports 68 pages, while standalone Part 03 reports 60 pages. Therefore, **Part 01 PDF pages 170 and 202 cannot be page indexes in the directly linked Part 01 PDF**.

This does not prove the quoted evidence is false. The numbers might refer to a larger combined scan, a separate copy, a page-image sequence, or a different document. The current report does not define that alternative pagination. Until the exact file and pagination are matched, those figures must not be used as verified standalone-PDF page indexes.

References:
- FBI Vault, [Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 01](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2001) — standalone PDF metadata reports 68 pages.
- FBI Vault, [Jeffrey Lionel Dahmer Part 03](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2003) — standalone PDF metadata reports 60 pages.

**Required final citation convention:** Record each of the following separately where available:
1. FBI Vault Part number and direct URL;
2. actual PDF page index (1-based), after checking the linked file;
3. printed page number written on the underlying document, if any;
4. document/form label and date.

Do not substitute one of these locator types for another.

## 2. Steven Tuomi — Ambassador Hotel chronology

### Verified source-text location

The FBI Vault Part 03 page extract identifies a continuation of an FD-302 interview of Jeffrey L. Dahmer dated August 3, 1992, with the document marked **Page 8**. It says Tuomi was Dahmer's second murder and places the incident at the Ambassador Hotel “somewhere around 1986 or 1987.” It also records Dahmer's account that he did not intend to kill Tuomi and said he awoke to find Tuomi dead.

**Direct source:** [FBI Vault, Part 03](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2003)

### Citation correction

The source location currently assigned in the AI report—Part 01, “PDF Page 202,” described as the interrogation transcript—is not verified as a page index in the standalone Part 01 PDF. The explicit web-accessible text for the approximate-year statement is in **Part 03**, on the continuation of the August 3, 1992 FD-302 marked Page 8.

Use the source above for the approximate-year recollection. The **standalone PDF page index remains to be checked**; do not invent it.

### Limits and wording

- The approximate date is Dahmer's retrospective statement, not independent proof of the precise date of death.
- An FBI transmittal describing offenses from November 1987 onward does not, by itself, disprove the approximate 1986/1987 recollection concerning an uncharged case.
- The September 15, 1987 date should be attributed to the specific source that states it and labelled according to what that source actually says (for example, last seen, last heard from, or reported missing). These terms are not interchangeable.
- The absence of a murder charge and the lack of recovered remains are separate facts requiring appropriate source support.

**Status:** Approximate-year statement located in primary-source text; precise PDF index pending.

## 3. Steven Hicks — age and 1978 chronology

### Verified FBI text

The FBI Vault Part 03 search-accessible field report is headed as a Cleveland Division investigation conducted on July 24, 1991. It records Hicks's date of birth as June 22, 1959 and discusses the 1978 Ohio case. This is consistent with Hicks being **18 on June 18, 1978**, four days before his nineteenth birthday.

**Direct source:** [FBI Vault, Part 03](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2003)

A separate FBI Vault item in Part 18 is a September 16, 1991 letter concerning a premortem head-and-neck radiograph for identity comparison. It also records Hicks's birth date as June 22, 1959 and describes him as believed to be Dahmer's first victim. This source concerns the forensic identity process, not proof of the exact last-seen date.

**Direct source:** [FBI Vault, Part 18](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2018)

### Citation mapping

- **Age 18 / date of birth:** FBI Part 03 supports DOB 22 June 1959; Part 18 corroborates the DOB.
- **Reported missing on June 24, 1978:** An FBI Part 01 memo states Hicks was reported missing to Summit County, Ohio authorities on June 24, 1978. That is a *reported-missing date*, not necessarily his last-seen date.
- **Last seen June 18, 1978 / hitchhiking to a concert:** Keep the source already mapped to this exact claim in the Victims plan, including the principal biographical retrospective. The cited FBI passage identified here does not, by itself, establish June 18 as the last-seen date.
- **Remains and identification:** The September 1991 forensic correspondence is relevant to the identification process; cite a specific report for the actual identification conclusion rather than implying the radiograph request alone completed it.

### Page-anchor limitation

The FBI report text identifies the underlying field report as `CV 7-MW-26057`, with an internal page marking of 1 in the available text extraction. However, the exact 1-based page index for this item in the standalone Part 03 PDF was not conclusively confirmed during this pass. Record the source as **Part 03, CV 7-MW-26057, report page marked 1; PDF index pending**, unless direct inspection of the linked file establishes the index.

**Status:** DOB/age support located; reported-missing and last-seen dates correctly distinguished; exact PDF index pending.

## 4. James “Jamie” Doxtator — age and date conflict

### Verified FBI text

The FBI Vault Part 01 search-accessible memorandum contains a page headed `PAGE TWO DE FBIMN 0011`. It describes James E. Doxtator as a male with date of birth **March 1, 1973** and says he was 14 when he disappeared in the spring of 1988.

**Direct source:** [FBI Vault, Part 01](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-lionel-dahmer/Jeffrey%20Lionel%20Dahmer%20Part%2001)

### Citation mapping and conflict

- **Age 14:** Supported by this FBI memo and consistent with the listed DOB for a disappearance in 1988.
- **Exact last-seen date January 16, 1988:** The cited FBI memo does **not** establish this exact date; it uses the broader phrase “spring of 1988.” The exact January 16 date remains mapped to the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel retrospective in the current Victims plan.
- **Chronology conflict:** January 16 falls in winter, whereas this FBI memo describes the disappearance as occurring in spring 1988. Preserve this as a source-level discrepancy instead of saying the FBI independently confirms January 16.
- **Ancestry / hobbies / family recollections:** The memo excerpt above does not substantiate these biographical details. Keep them mapped to the biographical reporting where the details appear, and retain the content plan's cautious attribution for ancestry.

### Page-anchor limitation

The earlier evidence report assigns a “Part 01, PDF Page 170” locator to other entries in this memo sequence. That cannot be the index of the standalone Part 01 PDF linked above, whose metadata reports 68 pages. The memo's “PAGE TWO” label is document pagination; it is not confirmation of standalone PDF index 170.

**Status:** DOB and reported age supported; exact last-seen date is not supported by this memo; discrepancy and page-index issue remain open.
